# Fixed-policy telescoping anytime UCB regret

Task id: `UCB-FIXED-POLICY-TELESCOPING-ANYTIME-REGRET`
Kind: `theoremFormalization`
Status: `compiled`
Harness: `hierarchical`

## Goal

Construct one measurable finite-arm UCB policy whose round-`t` score uses the
telescoping confidence share `delta / ((t+1)(t+2)K)`, with no terminal horizon
in the policy, state, score, or generated-action declaration.  On the single
canonical `Kernel.trajMeasure` generated by that policy, prove the all-time
confidence event, an all-horizon positive-gap pull-count bound, and a
finite-horizon expected pseudo-regret corollary.  A horizon may index a
consumer theorem or its deterministic bound, but may not change the policy or
trajectory measure.
