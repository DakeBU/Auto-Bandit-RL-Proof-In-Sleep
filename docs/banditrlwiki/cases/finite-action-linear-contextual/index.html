    Known gap. The upper differs from the lower by iterated logarithms; Theorem 2 also requires n at most 2^(d/2) and T at least d (log_2 n)^(1+epsilon).

    Lower bound guarantee. Formula renderer unavailable; readable fallback: Under the theorem's action-count and horizon fence, every algorithm has regret at the displayed finite-action contextual scale.\[R^*(T;n,d)=\Omega_\varepsilon\!\left(\sqrt{dT\log n\,\log(T/d)}\right).\]Swipe to read the full formula →

    For every small epsilon greater than zero, n is at most 2^(d/2) and T is at least d (log_2 n)^(1+epsilon), together with the source's remaining conditions.
