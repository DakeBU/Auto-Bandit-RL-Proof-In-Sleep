    Lean module · Tsallis-FTRL

    BanditRLProof.TsallisFTRLInteriority

    The square-root regularizer has an infinite inward slope at a zero coordinate. This module makes that boundary argument finite and algebraic: transfer a sufficiently small positive mass from any positive donor coordinate to the zero coordinate and contradict global minimality.
