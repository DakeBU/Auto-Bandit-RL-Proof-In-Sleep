    Lean module · Tsallis-FTRL

    BanditRLProof.TsallisRefinedImportanceWeightedMoment

    This module isolates the finite-sum calculation behind the ordinary importance-weighted part of the refined half-Tsallis stability route. The baseline is the sampled raw loss. The quadratic moment contracts to sum_a sqrt (p a) * (1 - p a), while the positive cubic remainder is at most one.
