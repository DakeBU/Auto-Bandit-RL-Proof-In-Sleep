    Lean module · EXP3

    BanditRLProof.Exp3MixedSquareBernstein

    The interval-sub-Gaussian route treats the mixed estimator square as an arbitrary variable in [0, 1 / epsilon], producing a variance proxy quadratic in the reciprocal exploration floor. Here the exact finite sampling law gives a centered second moment at most K / epsilon. A fixed-tilt conditional MGF argument then yields a generated finite-horizon Bernstein tail whose square-root term is linear, rather than quadratic, in that reciprocal floor.
