    Lean module · EXP3

    BanditRLProof.Exp3SampledHistoryScore

    This module closes the input-score boundary of Exp3RecursiveTrajectory for real-valued observed losses. The score at an inclusive finite history is the previous score plus the importance-weighted loss of the newly observed pair. The probability in that increment is exactly the exploration-mixed policy computed from the preceding score and history prefix.
