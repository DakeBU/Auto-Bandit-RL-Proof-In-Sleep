    Lean module · EXP3

    BanditRLProof.Exp3RecursiveTrajectory

    This module turns a measurable finite-history score into the exploration-mixed exponential policy used by EXP3. It proves normalization, coordinate measurability, and a uniform exploration floor, packages the policy as the project's stochastic finite-history algorithm, and invokes the Mathlib-backed Ionescu--Tulcea trajectory kernel. The resulting theorem identifies every successor action's conditional law with the explicit finite-action policy.
