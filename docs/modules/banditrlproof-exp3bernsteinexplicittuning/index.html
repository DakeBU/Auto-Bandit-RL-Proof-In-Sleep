    BanditRLProof.Exp3BernsteinExplicitTuning

    This module discharges the three dominance premises of the characterized 11 * gamma * T theorem with an explicit maximum of two cube-root scales and one square-root scale. The schedule is clipped at 1 / 2; transparent large-horizon premises ensure that the clip is inactive.
