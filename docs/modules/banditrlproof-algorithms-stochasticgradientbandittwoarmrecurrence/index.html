    BanditRLProof.Algorithms.StochasticGradientBanditTwoArmRecurrence

    This module instantiates the generated conditional Equation (8) at the two action-dependent coefficients used in the proof of Theorem 1 of Baudry--Johnson--Vary--Pike-Burke--Rebeschini (NeurIPS 2025). It proves both one-step exponential recurrences over the actual generated history-step kernel, under the source initialization theta = 0.
