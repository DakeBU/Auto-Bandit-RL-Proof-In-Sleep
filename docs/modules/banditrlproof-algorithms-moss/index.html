    BanditRLProof.Algorithms.MOSS

    Algorithm 7 in Lattimore--Szepesvari, *Bandit Algorithms*, uses a horizon dependent, realized-pull-count index. These definitions keep its factor four and log-plus truncation. They do not yet construct a stochastic history law or prove Theorem 9.1's regret bound.
