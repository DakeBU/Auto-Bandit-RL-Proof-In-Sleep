    Lean module · Tsallis-FTRL

    BanditRLProof.TsallisConjugatePotentialStability

    This module formalizes the deterministic potential quantity used in the paper-faithful refined Tsallis-INF stability route. The local learning-rate normalization is half the paper normalization, so the translated Lemma 19 coefficients are eta for the quadratic term and 2 * eta^2 for the positive cubic remainder.
