    BanditRLProof.Exp3MixedSquareConfidence

    This module replaces the Markov-only confidence step for the observed mixed importance-weighted estimator-square sum by a finite-action conditional sub-Gaussian route. The raw score lies in [0, 1 / epsilon] and its conditional mean is the armwise predictable loss-square sum. Consequently, the centered generated process has an exponential finite-horizon tail.
