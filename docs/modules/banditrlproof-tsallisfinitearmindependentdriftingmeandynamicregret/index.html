    Lean module · Tsallis-FTRL

    BanditRLProof.TsallisFiniteArmIndependentDriftingMeanDynamicRegret

    This module upgrades the fixed-baseline all-regimes theorem to the comparator that maximizes the actual reward mean at every round. The dynamic regret is decomposed into fixed-model.bestArm regret and the actual mean advantage of the moving comparator. The supplied armwise mean-deviation envelope controls that second term.
