    Lean module · Finite-horizon RL

    BanditRLProof.RL.FiniteHorizonNaturalCausalBoundedStoppingTimeHighProbabilityAverageRealizedBehaviorRegret

    The exact natural average realized behavior-regret process and its scheduled fixed-prefix logarithmic rate are evaluated at one positive bounded Mathlib stopping time. The stopped violation is measurable at the deterministic bound and is covered pathwise by the finite union of fixed-prefix violations.
