    Lean module · Tsallis-FTRL

    BanditRLProof.TsallisScheduledRefinedExpectedPenalty

    This module keeps the reciprocal-rate increments from the deterministic time-varying penalty theorem instead of collapsing them into a terminal potential mass. The mass above the point-mass baseline is eliminated in favor of suboptimal-arm terms, then transported through expectation by the compiled square-root Jensen bridge.
