    BanditRLProof.RL.FiniteHorizonStageTransitionJointFactorization

    This module proves that a fixed generated trajectory-stage joint transition mass factors into its state-action visit mass and the true MDP transition kernel singleton mass. The proof follows the recursive finite trajectory kernel; it does not divide by visit probabilities or introduce empirical confidence.
