    Lean module · Tsallis-FTRL

    BanditRLProof.TsallisFiniteArmIIDHistoryAdaptiveCorruptedRewardLaw

    The base reward vector is fresh IID at every round. Before the current action is sampled, a measurable arm-dependent reward shift may be selected from the preceding finite pair history. Shifted rewards are clipped to [0, 1]. A deterministic time-and-arm envelope controls the resulting baseline-gap perturbation and therefore supplies the additive regret budget.
