    Lean module · Tsallis-FTRL

    BanditRLProof.TsallisSqrtScheduleSelfBoundingRefinedScalar

    The local refined stability envelope has coefficient 5. After the change of variables used by the square-root schedule, its scalar objective therefore has a beta equation with offset 2, rather than the paper's idealized offset 1. This module records the corrected root interval and an elementary root estimate that does not require Lambert W.
