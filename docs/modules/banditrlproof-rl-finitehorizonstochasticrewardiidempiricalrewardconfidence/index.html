    Lean module · Finite-horizon RL

    BanditRLProof.RL.FiniteHorizonStochasticRewardIIDEmpiricalRewardConfidence

    This module retains the sampled reward in every generated episode record. It proves that a fixed stage/state/action reward sum, centered by its stored MDP mean and masked by the visit event, is sub-Gaussian across iid complete trajectories. The total proxy is the conservative episode-linear proxy episodes * varianceProxy; no bounded sampled-reward or exact-count claim is used.
