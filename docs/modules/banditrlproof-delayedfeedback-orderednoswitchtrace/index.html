    BanditRLProof.DelayedFeedback.OrderedNoSwitchTrace

    This module composes the source-faithful one-item transition from OrderedProcessingTransition.lean across an arbitrary finite trace. A trace may either process one member of B(t) \ S through the no-switch structural projection of Algorithm 5 lines 3--4 and 7--8, or close an exhausted inner loop and advance to the next action round.
