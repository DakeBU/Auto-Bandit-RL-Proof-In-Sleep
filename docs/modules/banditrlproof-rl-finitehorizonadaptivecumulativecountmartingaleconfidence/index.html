    Lean module · Finite-horizon RL

    BanditRLProof.RL.FiniteHorizonAdaptiveCumulativeCountMartingaleConfidence

    This module supplies the statistical producer required by the cumulative count-radius planner. Each raw episode-batch count is centered by its history-kernel integral. The exact adaptive iid batch law then gives a conditionally sub-Gaussian increment with the within-batch Bernoulli proxy, rather than the weaker whole-batch bounded-range proxy.
