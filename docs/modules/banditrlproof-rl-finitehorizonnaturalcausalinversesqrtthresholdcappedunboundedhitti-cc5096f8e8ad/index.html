    Lean module · Finite-horizon RL

    BanditRLProof.RL.FiniteHorizonNaturalCausalInverseSqrtThresholdCappedUnboundedHittingAfterStoppedAverageSampledReturnExpectedOptimality

    This module exposes the observed successor-batch sample means whose complement from the optimal initial value is the natural average realized-regret process. The empty prefix is assigned the optimal value, so the complement identity is total and remains valid at the WithTop.untopA fallback. The identity is then transported through the capped and genuine uncapped stopping prefixes and through Bochner integration. No expectation/stopping-index interchange or optional-stopping theorem is used.
