    Lean module · Thompson sampling

    BanditRLProof.Algorithms.ThompsonCanonicalSampler

    This module constructs the one-step joint law obtained by sampling an environment/history pair from a Bayesian prior-likelihood model and then sampling an action from the canonical posterior pushed through a measurable best-action selector. The resulting probability-matching theorem has no separate pair-law or action-law premise.
