    Lean module · Tsallis-FTRL

    BanditRLProof.TsallisScheduledAllRateExpectedStability

    This module supplies the coarse branch omitted by the refined scheduled stability theorem. Ordinary importance weighting has one-round expected conjugate-potential stability at most one for every positive local rate. The final generated-trajectory theorem combines that fallback with the refined bound available when the local ABRL rate is at most 1 / 2.
