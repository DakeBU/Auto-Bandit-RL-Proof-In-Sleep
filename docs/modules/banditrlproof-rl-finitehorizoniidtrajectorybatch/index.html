    Lean module · Finite-horizon RL

    BanditRLProof.RL.FiniteHorizonIIDTrajectoryBatch

    This module maps a finite product of genuine policy trajectory laws into the finite-batch empirical-model surface. It exposes both the episode/stage marginal law and independence across episode coordinates. The policy is fixed across episodes; adaptive cross-episode policy updates and concentration of visit-conditioned empirical ratios remain downstream.
