    BanditRLProof.Exp3PredictableMoments

    This file transports the fixed-environment canonical trajectory law through an environment prior. The resulting global joint law retains the latent environment in the conditioning history, which is the law surface needed to identify predictable feedback coordinates and their roundwise moments.
