    BanditRLProof.RL.FiniteHorizonNaturalCausalInverseSqrtThresholdCappedUnboundedHittingAfterL1TruncationEquivalence

    The capped double-linear scan and the genuine uncapped hittingAfter rule use the same deterministic base. Outside the existing capped delayed set, both rules stop exactly at that base and their stopped regret coordinates agree. Their difference also converges to zero in L1, in the named Lp Real 1 space, and in measure.
