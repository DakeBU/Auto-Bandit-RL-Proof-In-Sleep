# CUCB source model, deterministic triggering and impossible case

2026-09-18. Partial progress under contract `81a1998`; full performance endpoints and independent acceptance remain open, accepted topics 0/10.

`Algorithms/CUCBDeterministicTrigger` first proves a reusable actual-trajectory support theorem: a measurable round property holding almost surely under every input's actual round kernel holds almost surely at every coordinate of the infinite CUCB trajectory. It uses the genuine initial coordinate law, prefix/successor joint law and countable conjunction. The deterministic trigger proof then derives observation probability one from the primitive lower bound p_i=1, integrates through the actual oracle/environment mixture, and obtains N_i(n)<=T_i(n) almost surely for every n. It does not assert support on null feedback records, nor replace this exact source branch by a positive failure bound.

`Algorithms/CUCBFeedbackModel` assembles finite feasible superarms (an injective selected-arm-set map), nonempty possible-trigger sets, selected-arm inclusion and probability-one observation, triggerability of every retained arm, fixed bounded marginal laws and primitive ObservationCompatible, plus nonnegative integrable aggregate reward. Cross-arm dependence is allowed and realized reward has no uniform bound. `minTrigger` is the finite minimum of the actual environment observation probabilities over actions that can trigger the arm. Its positivity, <=1 bound and actionwise lower-bound property are proved. The exact nice event, probabilistic charged tail and deterministic branch instantiate with these computed parameters, not an unrelated supplied probability.

`Algorithms/CUCBSourceModel` adds the complete frozen primitive score/oracle specification: nonnegative scores agreeing with actual expected reward at the true input, coordinate monotonicity, bounded smoothness on possible-trigger arms, continuous strictly increasing nonnegative-domain modulus with value zero at zero, alpha/beta in (0,1], measurable randomized oracle with per-input alpha-success probability >=beta, and the disclosed positive-gap inverse range clause. The optimum is the maximum over the actual finite feasible family. Badness, signed action gap and inverse-gap parameter are derived, as is the ChargeData used by the actual normalized counter recurrence. Inverse positivity and exact modulus equality, optimum monotonicity, normalized threshold sufficiency and no-bad-action zero gap/count boundaries are proved.

This is the complete primitive model specification and parameter connection, not yet a finished performance theorem or a concrete noisy witness. The oracle trajectory conditional-success theorem is now proved below; the actual/mean reward expectation identity is now proved below.

`Algorithms/CUCBImpossibleCase` proves the source constant-six radius implication and the actual-index impossible case. On the exact empirical-mean nice event, an alpha-successful action cannot have positive true gap if every potentially triggered arm has T_i>6 log(t)/(f^{-1}(gap))^2. The proof uses the actual clipped CUCB index, its established optimism, a finite maximum over possible-trigger errors and strict monotonicity of the modulus. Strict threshold comparisons, zero observations and t=n+1 are preserved.

`Algorithms/CUCBGapInverse` extends the inverse from individual action gaps to the entire frozen interval (0,Delta_max]. It proves positivity, exact inversion, uniqueness, strict monotonicity and agreement with inverseGap at actual bad-action gaps. The exact piecewise sampling threshold is antitone in the gap and interval-integrable between any two positive source-domain endpoints (including equal/reversed endpoints). The zero default outside the domain is explicit and is not used to claim inversion there. This closes the scalar inverse/integrability prerequisite; it does not prove the refined regret integral bound itself.

`Algorithms/CUCBOracleMeasurable` derives score continuity from the existing bounded-smoothness/modulus assumptions using the finite input-space metric. It proves continuity of the finite optimum and measurability of the joint score and actual history/action success event. No additional measurability field is added to SourceModel.

`Algorithms/CUCBOracleSuccess` integrates the actual oracle action marginal, then lifts the primitive per-input alpha-success probability through the true initial and conditional successor trajectory laws. It proves conditional expected success indicator >=beta, the initial bound, and failure probability <=1-beta for every actual decision round, including the first. Integrability is produced from the indicator's [0,1] range; oracle success is not assumed independently after adaptive action selection. These are the probability producers needed for signed regret cancellation, not the final cancellation or regret theorem.

`Algorithms/CUCBRewardKernel` derives action-mixture and arbitrary measurable input-mixture reward integrability. Nonnegativity equates each environment L1 reward integral to its expected score, bounded by the finite true optimum. This bounds integrals only; realized rewards remain unbounded. It then proves the actual joint action/feedback reward integral equals the true-score integral.

`Algorithms/CUCBActualReward` transfers these results through the genuine initial and prefix/successor joint laws to every actual trajectory coordinate and every finite horizon. The defined approximationRegret is the original n*alpha*beta*opt minus expected actual cumulative reward. Its equality to the true-score expression and to expected signed action-gap sum minus n*alpha*(1-beta)*opt are proved, including zero horizon. This preserves the oracle-failure cancellation credit. Exact cancellation is now proved below; the full refined regret bounds remain open.

`Tests/CUCBSourceModelCanary` records dependency audits of these declarations. It is a dependency check, not the required concrete noisy final-performance canary. No independent semantic review or controlled experiment has been reported as complete.

`Algorithms/CUCBThresholdTail` converts the exact horizon-H sampling threshold into the actual fixed-count observation shortfall tail at decision time t=n+1<=H. For each count, it minimizes the positive inverse gap over the fixed finite eligible action family; that single tail covers every eligible action without a factor for the number of actions. The union over actual integer counters 0,...,n gives t^-2 per probabilistically triggered arm. The p_i=1 shortfall event has measure zero by the existing actual-law all-horizon counter/observation comparison. The finite arm union gives m/t^2, or zero when all arms have p_i=1. The global minimum p_* is computed from actual trigger probabilities; nonempty base arms are derived from the feasible family rather than added as a new assumption.

`Algorithms/CUCBSufficientSampling` connects this tail to the actual chosen action and normalized charge. On an alpha-successful oracle round, a bad action whose charged counter crosses the horizon threshold lies in the complement of the exact nice event or in the arm shortfall union. Its probability is at most (2+I{p_*<1})m/t^2, retaining the source constant and deterministic branch. No action-cardinality factor or post-selection independence assumption is introduced. This closes the per-round sufficient-sampling probability producer, not the cumulative regret theorem.

`Algorithms/CUCBRegretDecomposition` defines the actual under-sampled charged-gap weight, proves its nonnegativity and boundedness by Delta_max, and derives the pathwise signed gap decomposition. It proves measurability and integrability of actual under-sampled weights and sufficiently sampled success indicators. Taking expectations through the actual oracle-success producer and original signed regret identity cancels the negative oracle-failure credit exactly. No action selection independence or additional reward bound is used.

`Algorithms/CUCBRegretTail` identifies each sufficient indicator expectation with the actual event probability, bounds the finite inverse-square sum by the proved Basel value pi^2/6, and proves R(H)<=E(actual under-sampled weight)+(2+I{p_*<1})m*Delta_max*pi^2/6. Zero horizon is included. The under-sampled weight remains an actual random counter/gap quantity; the refined source integral bound on it is still unproved, so this is not yet Theorem1 or Theorem2.
