4 # Book Map Chapter 9 canonical Hoeffding UCBVI-CH canary
5 
6 This external canary keeps the full generated chain visible: finite-MDP
7 optimality, occupancy regret, cumulative state updates, aggregate transition
8 alignment, the previous-Q clipped recurrent planner, same-source confidence,
9 optimism, generated episode regret, counting, Bellman martingale control, the
10 frozen `20/250` high-probability terminal, and its `K H delta` expected
11 corollary.  The final two declarations are instantiated on a nondegenerate
12 two-state, two-action, horizon-two deterministic MDP with two generated
13 episodes and `delta = 1/2`.
14 
15 This is only the known-reward Hoeffding UCBVI-CH scope.  It does not certify a
16 Bernstein/minimax, stochastic-reward, or realized-return UCBVI theorem.
17 -/
