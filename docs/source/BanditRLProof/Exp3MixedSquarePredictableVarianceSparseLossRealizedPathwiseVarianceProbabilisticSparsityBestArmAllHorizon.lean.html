4 /-!
5 # Best-arm all-horizon pathwise-variance EXP3
6 
7 This module upgrades the fixed supported-comparator all-horizon tail to the
8 best supported arm in hindsight. The confidence budget is calibrated armwise
9 as `delta / K`. The fixed-comparator off-sparsityFailure tail is unioned over
10 the arms, so the common sparsity-failure event is added only once afterward.
11 The strengthened residual is `delta + mu(sparsityFailure)`, and its practical
12 consumer needs only `mu(sparsityFailure) <= ofReal epsilon`.
13 
14 The older `delta + K * mu(sparsityFailure)` and `epsilon / K` wrappers remain
15 available as compatibility APIs.
16 -/
