4 /-!
5 # Explicit exploration tuning for sparse EXP3 with two predictable variances
6 
7 This module closes gamma tuning for the sparse generated-regret theorem that
8 uses both the mixed-square predictable variance and the exact selected-loss
9 predictable variance. The first three exploration scales are shared with the
10 single-variance pathwise route. The additional selected-loss scale is
11 
12 `sqrt (S * log (4 / delta) / T)`.
13 
14 Under four transparent horizon contracts, the internally eta/gamma-tuned
15 threshold is at most `16 * gamma * T`. The exact sparsity-failure event is
16 charged once; the practical endpoint has failure budget `delta + epsilon`.
17 -/
