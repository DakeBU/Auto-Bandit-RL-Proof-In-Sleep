4 /-!
5 # Best-arm all-horizon sparse EXP3 with two predictable variances
6 
7 This module upgrades the exact double-variance fixed-comparator all-horizon
8 tail to the best supported arm in hindsight. Confidence is calibrated at
9 `delta / K`; the common support-sparsity failure event is removed before the
10 finite comparator union and added only once afterward.
11 -/
