5 # All-horizon predictable-variance EXP3 under probabilistic sparse losses
6 
7 The explicit probabilistic-sparsity schedule has its refined
8 `14 * gamma * T` threshold when four horizon inequalities make clipping
9 inactive. Outside that regime this module uses the strict `T + 1`
10 zero-probability threshold under exactly the same internal eta, gamma, and
11 generated trajectory measure.
12 
13 The resulting theorem covers every positive horizon and preserves the exact
14 support-sparsity failure residual. Its practical endpoint consumes
15 `mu(sparsityFailure) <= ofReal epsilon` and returns
16 `ofReal delta + ofReal epsilon`.
17 
18 The refined branch still uses the global `K * T` Markov envelope. This is not
19 a pathwise-sparse variance, best-arm first-order, Freedman, anytime, or ideal
20 EXP3.P theorem.
21 -/

29 /-- Regime in which all four components of the probabilistic-sparsity
30 predictable-variance exploration schedule are at most one half. -/
31 def probabilisticSparseLossPredictableVarianceLargeHorizonCondition
32     (K S T delta : Real) : Prop :=
33   4 * (S * Real.log K) <= T ∧
34     32 * (5 * K ^ 2 * Real.log K ^ 2 * Real.log (5 / delta)) <=
35       delta * T ^ 3 ∧
36     8 * (K * Real.log (5 / delta)) <= T ∧
37     8 * ((Concentration.intervalVarianceProxy 0 1 : NNReal) : Real) *
38       Real.log (5 / delta) <= T
39 
40 /-- All-horizon threshold for the probabilistic-sparsity route: use the
41 explicit large-horizon threshold in its valid regime and `T + 1` otherwise. -/
42 noncomputable def probabilisticSparseLossPredictableVarianceAllHorizonRegretThreshold
43     {Action : Type v} (arms : Finset Action) (horizon sparsity : Nat)
44     (delta : Real) : Real := by
45   classical
46   let gamma :=
47     probabilisticSparseLossPredictableVarianceClippedExplorationRate
48       (arms.card : Real) (sparsity : Real) (horizon : Real) delta
49   exact if probabilisticSparseLossPredictableVarianceLargeHorizonCondition
50       (arms.card : Real) (sparsity : Real) (horizon : Real) delta then
51     probabilisticSparseLossPredictableVarianceRealizedMarkovExplicitThreshold
52       arms gamma horizon sparsity delta
53   else
54     (horizon : Real) + 1
