7 This module upgrades the fixed-comparator all-horizon Bernstein mixed-square
8 tail to the best supported arm in hindsight. The fixed-comparator schedule is
9 calibrated at `delta / K`; all comparators share the same generated trajectory
10 measure, so a finite union gives total failure probability at most `delta`.
11 -/

19 /-- Best-arm Bernstein mixed-square all-horizon threshold. The underlying
20 fixed-comparator schedule receives the confidence share `delta / K`. -/
21 noncomputable def bernsteinSquareBestArmAllHorizonRegretThreshold
22     {Action : Type v} (arms : Finset Action) (horizon : Nat)
23     (delta : Real) : Real :=
24   bernsteinSquareAllHorizonRegretThreshold
25     arms horizon (delta / (arms.card : Real))

28 for every positive horizon. The finite comparator union spends `delta / K` on
29 each arm and therefore has total failure probability at most `delta`. -/
