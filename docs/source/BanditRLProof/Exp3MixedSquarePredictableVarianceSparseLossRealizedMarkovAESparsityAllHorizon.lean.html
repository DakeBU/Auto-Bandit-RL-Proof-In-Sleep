3 /-!
4 # All-horizon sparse-loss EXP3 under almost-everywhere sparsity
5 
6 This module replaces the universal pathwise sparse-support contract of the
7 existing all-horizon route by an almost-everywhere contract under the exact
8 generated trajectory measure. The large-horizon branch combines the compiled
9 raw sparse-loss tail with the tuned and explicit budget comparisons. The
10 complementary branch keeps the strict `T + 1` zero-probability fallback.
11 
12 The exceptional sparsity set has measure zero, so this transport spends no
13 additional failure probability. The result still uses the armwise aggregate
14 loss mass and Markov's polynomial confidence dependence; it is not a
15 best-arm first-order or Freedman/EXP3.P theorem.
16 -/
