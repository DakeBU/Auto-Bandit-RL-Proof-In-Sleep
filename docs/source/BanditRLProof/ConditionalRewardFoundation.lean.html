4 # Canonical conditional reward foundation
5 
6 This module closes the canonical `COND-EXPECT-REWARD` route for reward-only
7 `trajMeasure` processes.  A centered reward-kernel law and deterministic
8 historywise proxy ceilings yield, on the generated history filtration:
9 
10 * conditional expectation zero for every successor centered reward;
11 * a conditional sub-Gaussian MGF witness at the selected proxy ceiling;
12 * the finite-sum Azuma-Hoeffding upper tail.
13 -/

128 /--
129 The canonical conditional reward foundation as one theorem-facing endpoint.
130 
131 For every successor time it exposes both conditional mean zero and the
132 conditional MGF witness.  The same assumptions also yield the finite-sum
133 Azuma-Hoeffding upper tail for the zero-initialized centered process.  Thus
134 `Finset.range n` contains the deterministic slot `Y 0 = 0` and successor
135 rewards `Y 1, ..., Y (n - 1)`.  When the cumulative proxy is zero, Lean's
136 totalized division makes the displayed exponential bound equal to `1`; this
137 endpoint does not claim a sharper degenerate-variance bound.
138 -/
