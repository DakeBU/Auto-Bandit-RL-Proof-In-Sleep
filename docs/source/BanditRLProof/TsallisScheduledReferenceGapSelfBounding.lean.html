4 # Reference-gap self-bounding for predictable perturbations
5 
6 The action probabilities and regret are generated by `actualLoss`, while a
7 second predictable loss vector on the same trajectory supplies the stochastic
8 baseline gap law.  A pointwise bound between the two loss differences then
9 converts the reference expected-gap law into a self-bound for the actual
10 regret.  This is the generic bridge needed by history-adaptive corruption.
11 -/
