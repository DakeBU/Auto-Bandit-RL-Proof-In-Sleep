3 /-!
4 # Scheduled OFUL budget exhaustion from block-start forced positive actions
5 
6 The telescoping-confidence OFUL algorithm is deterministic conditional on its
7 finite pair history. This module exposes its selected action as a named
8 history function and transports the canonical successor-action law to an
9 aligned-window positive-cost contract.
10 
11 At history index `block * window`, the policy generates trajectory action
12 `block * window + 1`. Under `2 <= window`, that action lies in the aligned
13 half-open block. If this selector is a fixed positive-cost `forcedAction block`
14 for every history, the existing a.e. budget-reach theorem gives exhaustion by
15 `budget * window` and closes the canonical expected-regret/tail terminal.
16 
17 The forced-selector equality is an explicit policy-design contract. Ordinary
18 OFUL score maximization does not establish it, and this module does not modify
19 the OFUL policy or claim a full bandits-with-knapsacks theorem.
20 -/
