4 # Pure-Hedge cross-weighted EXP3 confidence
5 
6 This module proves finite-horizon confidence bounds for the pure-Hedge weighted
7 importance estimator, including the predictable-minus-observed direction needed
8 by the regret decomposition.  It first identifies the conditional
9 mean under the exploration action law, transports the latent predictable score
10 to the observed trajectory score, proves adaptedness, and applies the local
11 conditional sub-Gaussian finite-sum tail theorem.
12 
13 The bounded-range proxy is Hoeffding's interval proxy for `[0, K / gamma]`.
14 This closes the pure-q cross-weight concentration leaf; it does not control the
15 random estimator-square term in the sampled Hedge inequality.
16 -/
