4 # Expected scheduled half-Tsallis stability at all actual times
5 
6 This module joins the distinct time-zero and successor conditioning surfaces.
7 Its final left side is exactly the full `Finset.range (horizon + 1)` stability
8 sum consumed by the scheduled pathwise regret decomposition.
9 
10 All included local rates must lie in `(0, 1 / 2]`.  The coarse fallback for
11 larger early rates remains a separate leaf.
12 -/
