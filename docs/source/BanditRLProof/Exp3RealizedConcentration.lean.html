6 # EXP3 realized-loss concentration
7 
8 This module identifies the successor action law inside `condExpKernel` without
9 requiring a countable ambient action type.  It then freezes the predictable
10 environment/history coordinates and applies the bounded centered Hoeffding MGF
11 bound to the selected and realized one-step loss deviations.
12 -/
