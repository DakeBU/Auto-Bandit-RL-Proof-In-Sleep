4 /-!
5 # Explicit exploration tuning under probabilistic sparse losses
6 
7 This module closes the exploration-rate leaf for the generated realized
8 predictable-variance EXP3 route whose support-sparsity condition may fail with
9 positive probability. The global `K * T` loss-mass envelope makes the Markov
10 component
11 
12 `(5 K^2 (log K)^2 log(5 / delta) / (delta T^3))^(1/5)`.
13 
14 Together with the sparse base, Bernstein-confidence, and realized-deviation
15 components, a clipped maximum supplies every algebraic premise needed to
16 reduce the eta-tuned threshold to `14 * gamma * T`. The final theorem keeps the
17 exact sparsity-failure residual and exposes the practical `delta + epsilon`
18 endpoint under the same internally tuned generated measure.
19 
20 This is still a global-envelope Markov theorem. It is not a pathwise-sparsity,
21 best-arm first-order, Freedman, anytime, or ideal EXP3.P result.
22 -/
