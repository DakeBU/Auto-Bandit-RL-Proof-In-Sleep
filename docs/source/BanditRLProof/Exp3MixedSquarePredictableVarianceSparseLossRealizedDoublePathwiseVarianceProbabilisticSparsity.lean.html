5 # Sparse realized EXP3 regret with two pathwise variance budgets
6 
7 Outside the explicit sparsity-failure event, the mixed-square predictable
8 variance is bounded by `(1 / (gamma / K)) * (S * T)` and the exact
9 selected-loss predictable variance is bounded by `S * T`. Four confidence
10 events receive `delta / 4`, and the common sparsity-failure set is charged
11 exactly once.
12 -/
