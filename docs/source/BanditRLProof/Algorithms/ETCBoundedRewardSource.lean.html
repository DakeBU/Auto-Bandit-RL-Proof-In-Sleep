6 # ETC bounded reward source contract

10 environment, product space, kernel, or filtration.

16 /--
17 Source contract for the bounded-reward ETC wrong-commit route.
18 
19 This is intentionally a thin structure: it records trace-level reward-coordinate
20 independence, a.e. coordinate measurability, a.s. interval bounds, and exact
21 mean identities over the ETC exploration horizon.
22 -/
23 structure BoundedRewardTraceSource

32   indep :
