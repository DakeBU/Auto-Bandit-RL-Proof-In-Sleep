5 /-!
6 # Horizon-free telescoping-scheduled UCB
7 
8 This module defines one finite-arm UCB policy.  Its score at history index `t`
9 uses the summable confidence share
10 `telescopingConfidenceShare delta t / K`; no terminal horizon occurs in the
11 policy, state, score, or generated-action declarations.  Finite horizons occur
12 only in downstream count and regret consumers.
13 -/
