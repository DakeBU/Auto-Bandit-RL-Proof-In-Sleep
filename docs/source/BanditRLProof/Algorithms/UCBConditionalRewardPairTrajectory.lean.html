6 /-!
7 # Canonical pair-trajectory UCB confidence consumer
8 
9 This module consumes the canonical action/reward trajectory simultaneous
10 empirical-mean event in the random-width UCB score algebra. It proves a
11 fixed finite-arm/time large-gap event bound, a positive-gap chosen-arm
12 explicit-threshold tail/ENNReal expected pull-count bound, and the resulting
13 finite-arm explicit-threshold and textbook positive-gap ENNReal pseudo-regret
14 sums. It requires a `CenteredRewardKernelLaw`, but no caller selected-reward
15 trajectory law or reward-range premise. It does not prove anytime confidence,
16 an asymptotic normalization, or a Real/Bochner expectation endpoint.
17 -/
