20 Centered pairwise reward differences are measurable at time `t` with respect to
21 the shifted generated history filtration.
22

106 /--
107 Independence-based conditional mean-zero source for one centered reward
108 coordinate.
109 
110 If the sigma-algebra generated by the centered reward coordinate is independent
111 of the conditioning sigma-algebra, and the centered reward has global integral
112 zero, then its conditional expectation against that sigma-algebra is zero.  This
113 is a thin ETC-shaped wrapper around Mathlib's `condExp_indep_eq`; it does not
114 derive the required independence or integral identity from a reward kernel.

318 /--
319 For a fixed-commit ETC action trace, the shifted generated history filtration
320 is contained in the reward-only past sigma-algebra.
321 
322 The action generators are deterministic singleton preimages, hence either
323 `univ` or `empty`; the reward generators at filtration level `i` are exactly
324 coordinates `j <= i`.  This is the missing deterministic-action bridge from the
325 reward-only past independence leaf to the local `History.historyFiltrationSucc`.
