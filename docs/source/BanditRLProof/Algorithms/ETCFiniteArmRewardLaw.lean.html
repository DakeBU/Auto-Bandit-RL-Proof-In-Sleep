9 # Finite-arm reward laws for the generated-history ETC route
10 
11 This module turns bounded arm-indexed probability laws with the finite-bandit
12 model means into the centered reward-kernel contract consumed by the canonical
13 ETC trajectory theorem. It then exposes the resulting conditional
14 sub-Gaussian MGF directly, without an abstract centered-law or variance-ceiling
15 argument.
16 
17 It also assembles the complete selected centered-reward finite-sum tail, the
18 pairwise empirical-mean wrong-commit probability, and both max-gap and per-arm
19 Bochner expected-regret bounds for the generated ETC action under the canonical
20 trajectory. The first transport layer extends both bounds to external reward
21 processes with an equal exploration-prefix pushforward. The conditional-law
22 layer then derives that identity from an initial marginal plus successor
23 conditional-distribution laws for both bounds. The practical adapters state
24 the successor laws directly as the stationary laws of the scheduled
25 exploration arms for both bounds. The LML-shaped adapters further coarsen those
26 constant laws from complete action/reward histories to reward-only prefixes for
27 both bounds. The seed-shaped adapters then use exploration-action a.e. equality
28 to reduce action-dependent feedback kernels to those constant laws for both
29 bounds. These results do not prove the exact upstream LML theorem.
30 -/
