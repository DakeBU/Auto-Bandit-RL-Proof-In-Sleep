4 # Native Real ETC source-law adapter
5 
6 This module converts the action-selected feedback-law fields exposed by an
7 algorithm/environment sequence into the reward-only scheduled laws consumed by
8 the native Real exact ETC regret theorem. It does not import LML: the theorem
9 statement mirrors the relevant `IsAlgEnvSeq` fields so the remaining upstream
10 wrapper is limited to source-name and action/tie alignment.
11 -/

18 /--
19 External native Real exact ETC regret from action-selected initial and
20 full-history successor feedback laws.
21 
22 The exploration action identities turn each action-selected kernel into the
23 constant law of the scheduled round-robin arm. The complete action/reward
24 history is then projected to the reward-only prefix expected by the compiled
25 finite-prefix uniqueness theorem.
