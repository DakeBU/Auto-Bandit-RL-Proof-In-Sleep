7 /-!
8 # Two-arm SGB Theorem 2: deferred-decisions trajectory prefix
9 
10 The latent arm-stream coupling samples every reward coordinate before the
11 algorithm runs.  The native fixed-IID process samples only the reward selected
12 at each round.  Connecting those constructions requires a deferred-decisions
13 argument, not merely equality of one-coordinate marginals.
14 
15 This module proves the first process-level half of that adapter.  A visible
16 trajectory prefix through `n` depends only on latent reward coordinates with
17 pull index at most `n`.  It packages that dependence as a Markov prefix kernel
18 and gives the exact finite stream-box/visible-prefix mixture law.  It also
19 separates the compiled next-action factorization and pathwise selected-coordinate
20 readout.  A count-capped restricted-measure induction proves the exact
21 branch-locality contract and its unconditional branchwise product law.  A
22 countable branch partition now aggregates those laws into the exact one-step
23 selected-reward joint and conditional laws, both on the coupling and on its
24 visible-trajectory marginal.  Native-prefix identification, selected-IID laws,
25 future/no-return control, and trajectory uniqueness remain downstream.
26 Consequently, no full native trajectory-law equality, stopped-reward IID
27 statement, or Theorem-2 terminal is claimed here.
28 -/
