5 # Generated stochastic-gradient-bandit conditional Equation (8)
6 
7 This module composes the source-exact bounded-reward Equation (8) with the
8 actual action/reward kernel generated by the SGB history policy.  Its main
9 contract permits an action-dependent exponent `q`; this is the form needed by
10 the two-arm Theorem-1 proof, where the selected-arm branches use different
11 coefficients.
12 
13 Proof route and API ledger:
14 
15 * `integral_exp_mul_le_sourceEqEight_of_ae_abs_le_one` supplies Equation (8)
16   on each conditional reward fiber;
17 * `Thompson.measurableEnvironmentInitialPairKernel` and
18   `Thompson.historyStepKernel` expose the initial and successor
19   policy/reward `compProd` laws;
20 * bounded reward support and measurability of the real reward coordinate
21   produce the fiber and joint exponential integrability required by
22   `Measure.integral_compProd`;
23 * `historyAlgorithm_policy` and
24   `Exp3.integral_finiteActionMeasure_eq_sum` turn the outer integral into the
25   explicit softmax mixture, while `softmaxProbability_sum` supplies its
26   constant term.
27 
28 There is no independence premise: each reward law is conditioned on the
29 actual finite action/reward history and selected action.  The source support
30 contract remains exactly `[-1, 1]`.  The result is a one-step conditional
31 moment bound, not either Theorem-1 recurrence or a regret endpoint.
32 -/

48 /--
49 Action-dependent Equation (8) for the generated initial action/reward pair.
50 
51 Source-time fence: this kernel samples source round `t = 1` from the untouched
52 parameter `initialTheta`; consuming pair zero then constructs source
53 `theta_2`.  In the source Theorem 1, `initialTheta` is the zero vector.  This
54 base bridge is therefore separate from the successor bridge below.
55 -/

179 /--
180 Action-dependent Equation (8) under the actual generated SGB history-step
181 kernel.  The real reward coordinate is measurable by construction, so the
182 only reward-law premise needed beyond the Markov-kernel contract is its
183 almost-sure source support in `[-1, 1]`.
184 
185 Source-time fence: `historyParameter initialTheta eta n history` has already
186 consumed trace pairs `0, ..., n`, so it is source `theta_{n+2}`.  This
187 successor kernel samples source round `n+2`; consuming its new pair produces
188 `theta_{n+3}`.  Consequently it does not replace the initial-pair bridge above
189 when assembling a recurrence that begins at source `t = 1`.
190 
191 For the later `Fin 2` consumer with best-arm probability `p`, the positive
192 recurrence instantiates this generic coefficient by
193 `q 0 = 2 * eta * (1 - p)` and `q 1 = -2 * eta * p`; the inverse-odds
194 recurrence uses the pointwise negation.  Those recurrence simplifications are
195 deliberately left to the next layer.
196 -/
