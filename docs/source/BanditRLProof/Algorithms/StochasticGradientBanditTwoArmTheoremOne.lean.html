6 # Two-arm stochastic-gradient bandit: source Theorem 1
7 
8 This module closes Theorem 1 of Baudry, Johnson, Vary, Pike-Burke, and
9 Rebeschini, *Does Stochastic Gradient really succeed for Bandits?*
10 (NeurIPS 2025), for the paper's fixed two-arm IID reward model.
11 
12 The route stays on the generated Algorithm-1 trajectory.  It proves the
13 Equation-(5) conditional-expectation tower, the exact expected-parameter
14 telescope, the forward-potential Jensen/log bound, the Equation-(7)
15 success/failure decomposition, and the bridge from generated probabilities to
16 the regret of the actually sampled actions.  The terminal
17 `twoArmFixedIIDDirac_theoremOne` uses a Dirac environment prior and maps Lean
18 `tailHorizon + 1` to the source horizon `T`, including the uniform first
19 action.  Its right-hand side and hypotheses retain the source constants,
20 `0 < Delta < 1`, and `eta * sourceC eta < Delta` exactly.
21 
22 This module does not claim the paper's Theorems 2--4, a general-`K` endpoint,
23 or verification of the paper beyond this named theorem.
