5 # Sampled-pair UCB consistency for finite-arm sub-Gaussian laws
6 
7 This module instantiates the canonical sampled pair-trajectory asymptotic UCB
8 route from stationary action-indexed reward laws.  The fixed initial action is
9 paired with a reward sampled from its arm law, while all successor laws use the
10 context-independent Markov reward kernel.
11 -/

21 /-- Initial pair law obtained by attaching a fixed action to its reward law. -/
22 noncomputable def finiteArmSubgaussianInitialActionRewardMeasure
23     {K : Nat} (armLaw : Fin K -> Measure Rat) (defaultAction : Fin K) :
24     Measure (Prod (Fin K) Rat) :=
25   Measure.map (Prod.mk defaultAction) (armLaw defaultAction)
