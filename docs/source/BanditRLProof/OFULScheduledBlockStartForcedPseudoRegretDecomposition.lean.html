3 /-!
4 # Pseudo-regret decomposition for block-start forced telescoping OFUL
5 
6 This module separates the complete finite-horizon pseudo-regret of the
7 block-start forced policy into the initial action, forced successor actions,
8 and ordinary optimistic successor actions. The successor index `n` denotes the
9 history used to select action `n + 1`.
10 -/

254 /--
255 The complete pseudo-regret through action `horizon` is the initial gap plus
256 the forced and nonforced successor charges. This identity is pathwise and does
257 not require a probability or confidence assumption.
258 -/

264     (best : Fin K)
265     (window horizon : Nat)

267     canonicalStandardHighProbabilityPseudoRegret
268         thetaStar actionFeature best horizon trajectory =
269       (linearValue thetaStar (actionFeature best) -
270           linearValue thetaStar
271             (actionFeature
272               (Thompson.canonicalHistoryTrajectoryAction trajectory 0))) +
273         blockStartForcedSuccessorPseudoRegret
