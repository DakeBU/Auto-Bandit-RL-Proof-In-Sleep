4 /-!
5 # Explicit exploration tuning for sparse-loss predictable-variance EXP3
6 
7 This module closes the exploration-parameter leaf for the sparse-loss
8 predictable-variance Markov route. Besides the usual sparse arm, Bernstein
9 confidence, and realized-deviation scales, the Markov variance threshold
10 introduces the fifth-root scale
11 
12 `(5 K s (log K)^2 log(5 / delta) / (delta T^3))^(1/5)`.
13 
14 Under transparent large-horizon contracts, the clipped maximum of these four
15 scales is positive, at most `1 / 2`, and satisfies every algebraic premise of
16 the gamma-characterized theorem. The resulting generated realized-regret tail
17 has threshold `14 * gamma * T`.
18 
19 The result still assumes pathwise armwise sparse losses and uses Markov control
20 for predictable-variance overflow. It is not a best-arm first-order theorem.
21 -/
