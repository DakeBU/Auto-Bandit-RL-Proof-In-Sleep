7 # Expected suboptimal-arm bound for scheduled half-Tsallis FTRL

9 This module converts the generated pathwise refined half-power budget into a
10 deterministic expression involving square roots of expected action
11 probabilities.  It is the Jensen bridge between the compiled scheduled
12 expected-regret theorem and the self-bounding completion-of-squares consumer.
