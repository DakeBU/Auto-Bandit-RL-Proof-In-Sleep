4 # Unbounded stopping-time expected OFUL pseudo-regret decomposition
5 
6 This module removes the deterministic stopping-horizon bound from the expected
7 pseudo-regret interface. It retains the exact bad-event random-envelope
8 integral: controlling that term by `delta` requires an additional moment or
9 tail contract and is not a consequence of first-moment stopping-time
10 integrability alone. This is an event decomposition, not optional stopping.
11 -/

21 /--
22 Semantic finiteness and first-moment regularity for a `WithTop Nat` stopping
23 time. The a.e. finiteness field rules out interpreting `untopA` at `top`; the
24 integrability field controls the random all-round gap envelope.
25 -/
26 structure IntegrableFiniteStoppingTime
27     {Omega : Type v} [MeasurableSpace Omega]
28     (mu : Measure Omega) (tau : Omega -> WithTop Nat) : Prop where
29   finite_ae : ∀ᵐ omega ∂mu, tau omega ≠ ⊤
30   integrable_rounds :
31     Integrable (fun omega => (((tau omega).untopA + 1 : Nat) : Real)) mu
