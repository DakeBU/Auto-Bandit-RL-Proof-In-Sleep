3 /-!
4 # Finite-horizon realized EXP3 concentration
5 
6 This module shifts the generated predictable realized-deviation process by one
7 time step so that its deterministic zero initial value and every actual round
8 fit Mathlib's conditional sub-Gaussian sum theorem.  The resulting public
9 theorem bounds the full finite-horizon realized-minus-exploration-mixed loss
10 sum under a probability environment prior.
11 -/
