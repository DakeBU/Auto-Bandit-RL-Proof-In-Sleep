3 /-!
4 # Canonical pair-trajectory masked conditional reward tails
5 
6 This module specializes the canonical action/reward trajectory law to one
7 history-policy-selected arm and the generic predictable-variance concentration
8 interface.  It identifies the policy mask with the sampled successor action
9 almost everywhere on the canonical trajectory and rewrites the masked constant
10 proxy as an actual successor pull count.  It also normalizes positive exact
11 count fibers into empirical means, performs fixed-horizon count peeling, and
12 closes the finite arm/time union on the canonical trajectory.  It does not
13 prove uniform-time confidence, UCB, or regret.
14 -/
