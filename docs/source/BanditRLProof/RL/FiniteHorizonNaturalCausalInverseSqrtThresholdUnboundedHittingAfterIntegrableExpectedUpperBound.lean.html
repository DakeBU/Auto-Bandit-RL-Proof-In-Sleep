5 /-!
6 # Expected upper bound at an uncapped inverse-sqrt hitting time
7 
8 For each fixed threshold index, the genuine Mathlib `hittingAfter` has an L2
9 round count. Uniform deterministic-coordinate second moments therefore make
10 the exact stopped average realized behavior regret integrable. Finite-hit
11 membership then gives the expected threshold upper bound. This is a stopping-
12 fiber argument, not optional stopping, and no lower expectation bound is
13 claimed.
14 -/
