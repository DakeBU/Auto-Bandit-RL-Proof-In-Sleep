4 /-!
5 # Self-consistent-budget realized successor regret for actual sampled optimism
6 
7 This module replaces the coarse transition budget `rewardBound + 2 * rewardBudget`
8 by the exact fixed point generated by a contraction coefficient `q < 1`.  It
9 retains actual sampled rewards, adaptive policy selection, three independent
10 confidence shares, and the globally centered successor-return transport.
11 
12 The endpoint is fixed-window and excludes the initial batch.  It does not claim
13 an anytime, minimax, or complete UCB-VI rate; schedule-level control of the
14 contraction, reward radius, exploration charge, and return radius remains
15 downstream.
16 -/
