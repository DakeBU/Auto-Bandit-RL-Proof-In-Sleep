3 /-!
4 # Fixed-prefix high-probability logarithmic behavior expected regret
5 
6 This module upgrades the natural-prefix pathwise process to a fixed-prefix
7 high-probability statement on the genuine heterogeneous dependent causal
8 source.  The probability is inherited from the existing finite union of
9 selected count-and-reward empirical-model events; no new independence, MGF,
10 or concentration claim is introduced here.
11 
12 Outside the named model event, every actual exploratory successor policy is
13 bounded by the compiled causal planning rate.  Summing those coordinate bounds
14 and reusing the explicit finite-sum logarithmic envelope yields a measurable
15 one-sided violation event whose probability is at most the exact accumulated
16 model-confidence budget.
17 
18 Regularity: the probabilistic consumer retains finite nonempty Standard Borel
19 State/Action, a probability initial law, positive reward proxy, horizon, and
20 base visit floor, bounded mean rewards, uniform mean-compatible selected-reward
21 sub-Gaussianity, and exploratory path support.  Failure policy preserves the
22 actual exploratory behavior, one dependent source, actual sampled batches,
23 the natural `t` to successor-batch `t + 1` convention, scheduled budgets, and
24 behavior/recommendation separation.  This is fixed-prefix control of a random
25 behavior expected-regret process, not an anytime theorem or realized-return
26 regret.
27 -/
