3 /-!
4 # Burn-in tail high-probability natural causal realized behavior regret
5 
6 This module repairs the nonvanishing finite-prefix model budget in the
7 fixed-prefix logarithmic realized-regret route.  Model failures before a
8 deterministic `burnin` are paid for by the uniform `2 * horizon` behavior
9 regret bound.  Only the infinite model tail from `burnin` onward enters the
10 probability event.  The return component remains the fixed-prefix sum of
11 successor-batch-average deviations, with each batch divided by its own
12 positive scheduled episode count.
13 
14 The resulting event has exact budget
15 `tailModelFailureBudget burnin + ENNReal.ofReal returnDelta`.  No
16 independence between the model and return events is used.  This is a
17 fixed-`burnin`, fixed-`rounds` theorem.  An all-prefix consumer must still
18 choose a sublinear growing burn-in and a vanishing return share.
19 -/
