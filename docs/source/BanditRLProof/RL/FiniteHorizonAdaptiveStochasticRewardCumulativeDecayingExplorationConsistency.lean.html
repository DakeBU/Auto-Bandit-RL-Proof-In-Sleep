4 /-!
5 # Stochastic cumulative decaying-exploration realized consistency
6 
7 This module lifts the cumulative empirical-optimistic exploratory source to
8 complete stochastic-reward episode batches. Policy selection reads only the
9 known-reward projection of the sampled prefix. The first layer proves the exact
10 complete trajectory pushforward to the deterministic cumulative source; later
11 layers consume the deterministic decaying-exploration count certificate and
12 the globally centered stochastic return tail.
13 
14 Every schedule index still uses its own finite batch and infinite trajectory
15 space. No common-process, almost-sure, anytime, or reward-mean-estimation claim
16 is made here.
17 -/
