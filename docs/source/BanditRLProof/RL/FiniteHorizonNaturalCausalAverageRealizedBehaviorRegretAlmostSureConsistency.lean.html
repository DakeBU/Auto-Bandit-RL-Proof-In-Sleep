5 /-!
6 # All-prefix almost-sure natural causal average realized behavior regret
7 
8 This module strengthens the fourth-power-prefix almost-sure theorem to every
9 deterministic natural prefix for the same per-batch-normalized,
10 equal-round-weighted process on the one heterogeneous causal trajectory
11 measure.
12 
13 The behavior term is the Cesaro average of the compiled almost-everywhere
14 successor-policy expected-regret limit. The return term uses the existing
15 fixed-prefix conditional-sub-Gaussian tail at rounds `n + 1` with summable
16 share `1 / (n + 2)^2`; first Borel-Cantelli needs no event independence.
17 
18 This is not an anytime confidence sequence, stopping-time theorem, raw
19 single-episode process, behavior/recommended-policy equality, minimax rate,
20 reachability theorem, or complete UCB-VI.
21 -/
