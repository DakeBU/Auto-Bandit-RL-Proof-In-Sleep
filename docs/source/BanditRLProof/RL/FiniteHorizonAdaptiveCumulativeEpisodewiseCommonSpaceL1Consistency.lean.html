5 # Common-space L1 realized behavior consistency
6 
7 This module packages the compiled expected absolute realized-regret convergence
8 in Mathlib's native `MemLp`, `eLpNorm`, and `Lp` interfaces.  The underlying
9 probability space is still the independent product of complete scheduled
10 finite-window experiments; no nested causal coupling is constructed here.
