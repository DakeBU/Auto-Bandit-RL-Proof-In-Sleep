3 /-!
4 # Natural causal L1 consistency for heterogeneous scheduled batches
5 
6 This route strengthens the compiled convergence-in-probability theorem on the
7 single heterogeneous causal trajectory measure. It does not use the independent
8 product of complete finite-window experiments.
9 
10 The expected selected-policy regret is uniformly bounded by `2 * horizon`.
11 After a fixed burn-in, the compiled model certificate controls its good-event
12 part, while the summable model tail pays the bounded bad-event contribution.
13 The globally centered sampled-return deviation is integrated directly from its
14 conditional sub-Gaussian MGF and divided by the actual successor episode mass.
15 
16 Regularity is unchanged: finite nonempty Standard Borel State/Action spaces, a
17 probability initial law, positive horizon/base visit floor/reward proxy, bounded
18 stored means, a uniform mean-compatible selected-reward sub-Gaussian law, and
19 the existing full-exploration path floor.
20 
21 Failure policy: preserve the one dependent causal source, actual coordinate
22 batch sizes, `n`-prefix to `n + 1` policy selection, successor-only initial
23 exclusion, global centering, and actual-mass normalization. No pathwise,
24 almost-sure, anytime, minimax, state-reachability, or complete UCB-VI statement
25 is inferred.
26 -/
