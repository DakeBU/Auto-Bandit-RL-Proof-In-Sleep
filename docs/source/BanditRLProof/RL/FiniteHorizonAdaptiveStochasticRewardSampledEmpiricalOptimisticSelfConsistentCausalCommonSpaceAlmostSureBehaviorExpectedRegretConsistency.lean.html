4 # Natural-causal almost-sure behavior expected-regret consistency
5 
6 This module proves that the actual exploratory successor policy's expected
7 regret tends to zero along almost every trajectory of the one heterogeneous
8 causal source.  The key project-local leaf extracts a pointwise behavior-
9 policy rate from the existing coordinate confidence proof.  First
10 Borel-Cantelli supplies eventual model-goodness, and the deterministic causal
11 planning rate then squeezes the nonnegative behavior regret to zero.
12 
13 Regularity is unchanged from the almost-sure realized-regret parent: finite
14 nonempty Standard Borel State/Action, probability initial law, positive
15 horizon, reward proxy and base visit floor, bounded stored means, uniform
16 mean-compatible selected-reward sub-Gaussianity, and full-exploration path
17 support.
18 
19 Failure policy: preserve the one dependent source, actual sampled coordinates,
20 `n`-prefix to `n+1` behavior selection, scheduled budgets, and the distinction
21 between the empirical model's recommended policy and the source's exploratory
22 successor policy.  The behavior expected-regret process is not claimed
23 measurable here, so this route proves an a.e. pathwise limit but no new
24 in-measure or `Lp` statement.  It also gives no every-trajectory, anytime,
25 reachability, minimax, or complete-UCB-VI result.
26 -/
