4 /-!
5 # Common-space consistency for actual-sampled self-consistent optimism
6 
7 Target theorem route: place the complete actual-sampled scheduled experiments
8 on one exact-marginal dependent product space and prove that realized successor-
9 average regret converges to zero in Mathlib `TendstoInMeasure`.
10 
11 Supporting obligations are:
12 
13 1. strengthen the finite-window terminal from a one-sided realized bound to an
14    absolute bound using the model-good expected-regret certificate and the
15    return-good two-sided deviation certificate;
16 2. construct the scheduled window source/law and its `Measure.infinitePi`
17    coupling;
18 3. transport the measurable bad event and regret bound through coordinate
19    evaluation; and
20 4. squeeze the distance event by the explicit failure and regret envelopes.
21 
22 Local APIs/imports are the compiled self-consistent explicit-rate terminal,
23 the self-consistent expected-regret occupancy identity, the stochastic realized
24 decomposition, `Measure.infinitePi_map_eval`,
25 `measurePreserving_eval_infinitePi`, and `tendstoInMeasure_iff_dist`.
26 Retrieval found no existing actual-sampled self-consistent common-space route;
27 the general evidence is project-local plus `MLIB-PROBABILITY-KERNEL`,
28 `MLIB-MEASURE-INTEGRAL`, `MLIB-METRIC-TOPOLOGY`, and `MLIB-ASYMPTOTICS`.
29 
30 Regularity is unchanged from the finite terminal: finite nonempty measurable
31 State/Action with equality and measurable singletons, Standard Borel
32 State/Action, a probability initial law, positive horizon/base floor/reward
33 proxy, a uniform selected-reward sub-Gaussian law, bounded means, and a full-
34 exploration path floor.
35 
36 Failure policy: preserve actual sampled rewards, successor indexing, initial
37 batch exclusion, all three confidence shares, global centering, and
38 `episodes * rounds` normalization. The product coupling below has exact window
39 marginals but is intentionally independent across complete windows. It is not
40 a nested causal stream and proves no pathwise, almost-sure, anytime, minimax,
41 state-reachability, or complete-UCB-VI claim.
42 -/
