3 /-!
4 # Episodewise adaptive realized behavior consistency
5 
6 This module sharpens the finite-window realized-return route by preserving the
7 iid structure inside each generated batch.  Complete episodes are independent;
8 stages inside one episode are not.  Centering one bounded full-episode return at
9 a time gives the batch proxy `episodes * horizon^2`, replacing the coarse
10 whole-batch proxy `(episodes * horizon)^2`.
11 
12 The sharper batch MGF is transported through the existing adaptive successor
13 conditional law and strongly-adapted finite-sum concentration route.  The
14 terminal theorem remains an indexed family of finite-window certificates over
15 changing batch and trajectory spaces, not a common-process convergence result.
16 -/

209     ProbabilityTheory.HasSubgaussianMGF

211         EpisodeBatch.totalReturn batch -

213             EpisodeBatch.totalReturn)

215       (policy.iidEpisodeBatchMeasure initialState episodes) := by
