5 # Natural-causal finite-prefix cumulative and average behavior regret
6 
7 This module sums the actual `source.successorPolicyAt` expected-regret process
8 over `Finset.range rounds` on the one genuine heterogeneous dependent causal
9 trajectory measure. `ExpectationBochnerSums.integral_finset_sum` identifies the
10 integral of that pathwise finite sum with the sum of coordinate expectations;
11 coordinate nonnegativity then identifies those terms with the compiled expected
12 absolute regrets. Summing the explicit integrated coordinate bounds gives a
13 finite-prefix cumulative rate, and division by `rounds` gives its Cesaro rate.
14 The existing `tendsto_natWeightedAverage_zero` theorem at unit natural weights
15 proves that this deterministic average rate tends to zero, so a nonnegative
16 squeeze yields average behavior expected-regret consistency.
17 
18 Regularity for the finite-sum identity is only the probability initial law and
19 the deterministic reward bound needed by coordinate integrability. The rate
20 consumer retains finite nonempty Standard Borel State/Action, a probability
21 initial law, positive horizon/base floor/reward proxy, bounded means, uniform
22 mean-compatible selected-reward sub-Gaussianity, and exploratory path support.
23 
24 Failure policy: preserve the actual exploratory behavior rather than the
25 recommended policy, the same dependent source and sampled batches, natural
26 coordinate `t` selecting successor batch `t + 1`, scheduled budgets, initial
27 batch exclusion, and the two model-confidence shares already integrated by the
28 parent. This route proves a finite-prefix cumulative bound and a vanishing
29 Cesaro average. It does not provide a closed sublinear cumulative rate,
30 realized-return control, every-trajectory or anytime guarantees, reachability,
31 minimax/optimal rates, or complete UCB-VI.
32 -/
