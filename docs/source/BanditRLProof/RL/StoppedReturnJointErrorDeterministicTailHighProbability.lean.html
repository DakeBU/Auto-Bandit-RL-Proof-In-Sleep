3 /-!
4 # Scalar joint-error deterministic-tail stopped-return optimality
5 
6 This module replaces the six-coordinate conjunction-only confidence surface by
7 one measurable scalar random variable: the maximum of the capped/uncapped
8 sampled-return, actual successor-policy-return, and same-prefix-gap errors.
9 The accepted deterministic-tail certificate then transports to direct strict
10 sublevel and weak superlevel events for this joint error.
11 
12 The cutoff remains existential and noncomputable. No convergence rate,
13 independence, delta upper bound, or optional-stopping argument is introduced.
14 -/
