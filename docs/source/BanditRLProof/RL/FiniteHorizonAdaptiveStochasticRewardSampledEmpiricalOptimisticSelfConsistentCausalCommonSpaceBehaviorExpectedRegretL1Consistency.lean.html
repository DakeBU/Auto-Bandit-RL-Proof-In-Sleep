4 /-!
5 # Natural-causal behavior expected-regret L1 consistency
6 
7 This module upgrades the actual exploratory `source.successorPolicyAt`
8 expected-regret process from a.e./in-measure convergence to `L1` on the same
9 genuine heterogeneous dependent causal trajectory measure. Every coordinate
10 is measurable, nonnegative, and bounded by the deterministic `2 * horizon`
11 policy envelope. Mathlib dominated convergence therefore gives expected
12 absolute convergence directly; no realized-return MGF, independent-window
13 coupling, or extra uniform-integrability assumption is used.
14 
15 Lean packages coordinate `Integrable` and `MemLp 1`, the exact exponent-one
16 `eLpNorm`, a named `Lp Real 1` process tending to zero, the existing
17 `TendstoInMeasure`, and a joint behavior-expected/realized `L1` terminal on the
18 same source. Coordinate integrability only needs the finite measurable source
19 contracts, a probability initial law, and bounded mean rewards. Convergence
20 retains the a.e. parent's finite nonempty Standard Borel State/Action,
21 positive horizon/base visit floor/reward proxy, uniform mean-compatible
22 selected-reward sub-Gaussianity, and full-exploration path support.
23 
24 Failure policy: preserve the actual exploratory behavior policy, the
25 recommendation/behavior distinction, one dependent source, actual samples,
26 `n`-prefix to `n+1` selection, scheduled budgets, initial exclusion, and the
27 separate realized-return centering. This proves behavior expected-regret
28 `L1`, but not an explicit integrated finite-round rate, convergence on every
29 trajectory, anytime control, state reachability, minimax/optimal rates, or
30 complete UCB-VI.
31 -/
