6 # Empirical-model confidence on the heterogeneous causal source
7 
8 This module transports actual sampled count-and-reward empirical-model events
9 to the dependent source whose coordinate `t` contains `episodes t` complete
10 stochastic episodes.  Unlike the constant-window route, local count and reward
11 shares are functions of the coordinate.  The fixed-prefix failure budget is
12 therefore their genuine finite `ENNReal` sum.
13 
14 Coordinate zero is generated by the initial exploratory policy.  Coordinate
15 `n + 1` is generated by the policy selected from the prefix through `n`.
16 Exact iid fibers and the prefix/next `compProd` law give each coordinate its
17 own model-event bound without assuming independence between adaptive rounds.
18 
19 Regularity is finite measurable nonempty State/Action with measurable
20 singletons, a probability initial law, Standard Borel State/Action for the iid
21 model-confidence parent, positive batch sizes and reward proxy, and positive
22 local shares at most one.  Concrete event measurability additionally uses the
23 compiled measurable heterogeneous optimistic-table selector.
24 
25 Failure policy: preserve actual sampled rewards, coordinate-specific batch
26 sizes and shares, generating-policy indexing, and the finite failure-budget
27 sum.  No constant-window rate, independence across rounds, uniform-time,
28 pathwise, almost-sure, anytime, minimax, reachability, or complete-UCB-VI claim
29 is inferred.
30 -/

143 theorem trajectoryMeasure_successorBadEvent_le
144     {mdp : MDP State Action} {initialState : Measure State}
145     [IsProbabilityMeasure initialState] {episodes : Nat -> Nat}

148     {bad : Set (HeterogeneousStochasticEpisodeBatchPrefix mdp episodes n ×

154   let prefixMeasure :=
155     source.trajectoryMeasure.map (Preorder.frestrictLe n)
156   let pairMap : HeterogeneousStochasticEpisodeBatchTrajectory mdp episodes ->
157       HeterogeneousStochasticEpisodeBatchPrefix mdp episodes n ×

160       (Preorder.frestrictLe n trajectory, trajectory (n + 1))

163     dsimp only [prefixMeasure]
