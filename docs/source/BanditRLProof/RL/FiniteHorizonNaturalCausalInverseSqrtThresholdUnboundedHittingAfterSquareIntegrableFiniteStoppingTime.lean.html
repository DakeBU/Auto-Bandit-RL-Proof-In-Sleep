4 /-!
5 # Second moment of the uncapped inverse-sqrt hitting time
6 
7 This module upgrades each fixed-index genuine `hittingAfter` first passage
8 from a first moment to `OFUL.SquareIntegrableFiniteStoppingTime` when
9 `4 < mdp.horizon`. Squaring the fourth-power checkpoint values produces a
10 seventh-degree block weight. The stronger horizon contract supplies an
11 inverse-tenth local confidence share, leaving a summable inverse-square
12 diagonal after shifted-tail reindexing.
13 
14 The result is fixed-index L2 regularity. It does not prove a uniform moment
15 rate, a stopped-process L1 theorem, an exponential crossing tail, or an
16 optional-stopping identity.
17 -/
