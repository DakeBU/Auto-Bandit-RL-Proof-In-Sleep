20 /-!
21 This theorem route slows the moving first-passage threshold from `1/(n+1)`
22 to `1/sqrt(n+1)`. Dividing the compiled inverse-cubic plus inverse-square L1
23 envelope by that threshold gives shifted p-series with exponents `5/2` and
24 `3/2`. Their summability permits first Borel-Cantelli and an almost-sure
25 eventual exact base-stop conclusion. No independence or optional-stopping
26 argument is used.
27 -/
