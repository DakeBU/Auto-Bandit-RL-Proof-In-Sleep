3 /-!
4 # Threshold-triggered double-linear raw-window stopping-time L1 consistency
5 
6 At schedule index `n`, this module observes the compiled natural average
7 realized behavior-regret process at the fourth-power prefix `(n+1)^4`. It
8 stops at that prefix when the observation is at most a deterministic threshold
9 and otherwise waits exactly `2*n+1` additional raw prefixes.
10 
11 The early-stop event is measurable in the exact natural filtration at the base
12 prefix, so Mathlib's piecewise-constant constructor yields a genuine stopping
13 time. The rate-controlled raw-window parent then gives the full L1,
14 in-measure, and almost-everywhere consistency package. No optional-stopping
15 identity or independence assumption is used.
16 -/
