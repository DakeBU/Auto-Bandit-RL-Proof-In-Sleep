3 /-!
4 # Stopped sampled and successor-policy return convergence modes
5 
6 This module upgrades the exact capped and uncapped stopped-return `L1` route
7 to literal convergence in measure and almost-sure convergence.  The sampled
8 return and the trajectory-law expected return of the actually selected
9 successor policies converge to the optimal initial expected return, while
10 their same-prefix difference converges to zero.
11 
12 The proof only transports already compiled process identities, `L1` limits,
13 and almost-sure regret limits.  It does not interchange expectation with a
14 random stopping index and does not use optional stopping.
15 -/
