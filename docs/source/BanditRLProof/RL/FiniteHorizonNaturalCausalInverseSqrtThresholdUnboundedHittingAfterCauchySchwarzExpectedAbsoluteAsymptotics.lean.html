4 /-!
5 # Cauchy--Schwarz degree-four expected-absolute asymptotics
6 
7 This module replaces the Young-inequality absolute-first-moment envelope by a
8 Cauchy--Schwarz estimate on the stopping fibers. The actual stopping-round
9 second moment still uses the accepted degree-eight polynomial envelope, while
10 its square root yields a degree-four expected-absolute growth bound.
11 
12 All model and source parameters are fixed as the threshold index varies. The
13 result is not optional stopping, uniform integrability, L1 convergence, or a
14 claim that the polynomial exponent is sharp.
15 -/
