3 /-!
4 # Explicit tail start for the uncapped inverse-sqrt hitting time
5 
6 This module replaces the convergence-selected tail start in the fixed-index
7 second-moment route by a concrete ceiling expression. It then transports that
8 explicit witness into the deterministic stopped-regret absolute-moment budget.
9 
10 The result remains fixed-index. It does not establish a uniform moment rate,
11 uniform integrability, L1 convergence, or an optional-stopping identity.
12 -/
