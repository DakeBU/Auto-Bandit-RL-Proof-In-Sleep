3 /-!
4 # Scheduled one-sided upper-tail consistency in probability
5 
6 This module consumes the explicit fourth-power prefix high-probability
7 terminal. For every fixed positive threshold, the deterministic regret
8 envelope is eventually below that threshold, so the threshold violation is
9 contained in the compiled envelope violation and inherits its vanishing exact
10 failure budget.
11 
12 The result is one-sided and only follows the deterministic fourth-power prefix
13 subsequence. It is not absolute TendstoInMeasure, all-prefix, or anytime
14 control.
15 -/
