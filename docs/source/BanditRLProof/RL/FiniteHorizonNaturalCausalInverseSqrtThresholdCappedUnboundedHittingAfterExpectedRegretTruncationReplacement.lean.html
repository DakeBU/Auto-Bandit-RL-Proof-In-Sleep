5 # Expected-regret replacement for capped and uncapped hittingAfter rules
6 
7 This module transports the accepted capped/uncapped `L1` truncation
8 equivalence through the Bochner integral. The result compares signed expected
9 stopped average realized behavior regret on the same generated trajectory law.
10 It is not an optional-stopping or policy-value identity.
