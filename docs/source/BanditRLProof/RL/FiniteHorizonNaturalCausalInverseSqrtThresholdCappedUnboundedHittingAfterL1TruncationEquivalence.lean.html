4 /-!
5 # L1 truncation equivalence for capped and uncapped inverse-sqrt first passage
6 
7 The capped double-linear scan and the genuine uncapped `hittingAfter` rule use
8 the same deterministic base. Outside the existing capped delayed set, both
9 rules stop exactly at that base and their stopped regret coordinates agree.
10 Their difference also converges to zero in `L1`, in the named `Lp Real 1`
11 space, and in measure.
12 
13 The capped delayed set is only used as a support certificate for possible
14 mismatch. It is not identified with an uncapped delayed event.
15 -/
