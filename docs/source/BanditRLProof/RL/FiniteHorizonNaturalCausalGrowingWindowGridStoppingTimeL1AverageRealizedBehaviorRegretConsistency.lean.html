5 /-!
6 # Growing-window grid stopping-time L1 natural causal consistency
7 
8 This module transports the compiled natural-causal L1 process through stopping
9 families whose values lie in a growing finite window of the fourth-power prefix
10 grid.  Every finite candidate sum is dominated by the infinite tail of the
11 compiled summable grid envelope, so the window width may grow arbitrarily.
12 
13 The result is restricted to the explicit fourth-power grid.  It does not prove
14 L1 convergence for an arbitrary growing interval of raw natural prefixes, and
15 it uses neither an optional-stopping identity nor independence.
16 -/
