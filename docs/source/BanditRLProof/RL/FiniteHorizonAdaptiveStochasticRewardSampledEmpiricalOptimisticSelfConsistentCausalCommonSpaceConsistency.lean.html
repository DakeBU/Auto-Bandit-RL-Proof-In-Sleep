5 /-!
6 # Natural common-space consistency for the heterogeneous causal source
7 
8 Target theorem route: prove convergence in probability of realized successor-
9 average regret on the single infinite causal sampled trajectory, rather than
10 coupling separate finite-window experiments.
11 
12 The proof uses four compiled surfaces: the heterogeneous trajectory law and
13 coordinate fibers, actual sampled-model confidence, the exact weighted
14 realized-regret decomposition, and the successor global-return sub-Gaussian
15 tail.  Its new ingredients are a summable tail model event, dilution of a
16 fixed burn-in prefix by the actual successor episode mass, and a return share
17 that vanishes slowly enough to preserve a vanishing confidence radius.
18 
19 Regularity is finite nonempty measurable State/Action with measurable
20 singletons, Standard Borel State/Action, a probability initial law, positive
21 horizon, reward proxy and base visit floor, bounded mean rewards, a uniform
22 selected-reward sub-Gaussian law, and the existing full-exploration path floor.
23 
24 Failure policy: preserve the single causal trajectory, actual coordinate
25 batch sizes, successor indexing, selected-policy fibers, two-sided global
26 return event, and exact weighted normalization.  No independence between
27 coordinates, almost-sure or pathwise convergence, anytime/minimax rate,
28 state-reachability theorem, or complete UCB-VI claim is inferred.
29 -/
