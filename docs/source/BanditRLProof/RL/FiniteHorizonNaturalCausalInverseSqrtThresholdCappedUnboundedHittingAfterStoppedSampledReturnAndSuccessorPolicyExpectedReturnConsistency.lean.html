4 # Stopped sampled-return and successor-policy expected-return consistency
5 
6 This module gives a literal policy-value interpretation to the stopped
7 sampled-return theorem.  At coordinate `t`, the successor policy is the actual
8 exploratory policy selected from the dependent prefix through `t`; its expected
9 return is the integral of cumulative reward under its generated trajectory
10 law.  These literal policy returns are averaged over the same natural prefix
11 as the sampled-return and regret processes, with the optimal initial expected
12 return at the empty prefix.
13 
14 The exact same-prefix identity says that sampled return minus successor-policy
15 expected return is the normalized return deviation.  It is transported to the
16 capped first-passage approximation and the genuine uncapped `hittingAfter`
17 prefix, then through Bochner integration.  No expectation is interchanged with
18 a random stopping index, and no optional-stopping theorem is used.
19 -/
