3 /-!
4 # Stopped sampled and successor-policy return L1 optimality
5 
6 This module upgrades the stopped expectation-level consistency theorem to
7 actual `L1` convergence.  The sampled-return optimality error is exactly the
8 negative realized behavior regret, the literal successor-policy return error
9 is exactly the negative behavior expected regret, and their same-prefix gap is
10 exactly the return deviation.  The already compiled capped and genuine
11 uncapped `hittingAfter` `L1` results therefore transport to all three return
12 processes.
13 
14 The successor-policy process remains the literal trajectory-law expected
15 return of the policy selected from the dependent prefix.  No expectation is
16 interchanged with a random stopping index, and no optional-stopping theorem is
17 used.
18 -/

36 /-- Eta-expanded negation has the same `eLpNorm`.  This small wrapper keeps the
37 return-process transport proofs independent of the syntactic representation of
38 pointwise negation. -/
39 theorem eLpNorm_fun_neg {Omega : Type*} [MeasurableSpace Omega]
40     (f : Omega -> Real) (p : ENNReal) (mu : Measure Omega) :
41     eLpNorm (fun omega => -f omega) p mu = eLpNorm f p mu := by
42   change eLpNorm (-f) p mu = eLpNorm f p mu
43   exact eLpNorm_neg f p mu
