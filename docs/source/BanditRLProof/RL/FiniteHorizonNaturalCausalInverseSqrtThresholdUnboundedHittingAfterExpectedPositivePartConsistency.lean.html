4 # Expected positive-part consistency at an uncapped inverse-sqrt hitting time
5 
6 The exact stopped average realized behavior regret can be negative. This file
7 isolates its one-sided excess, proves that its expectation is bounded by the
8 hit threshold, and sends that threshold to zero. The argument uses the exact
9 at-hit inequality and finite-measure integration, not optional stopping.
10 -/
