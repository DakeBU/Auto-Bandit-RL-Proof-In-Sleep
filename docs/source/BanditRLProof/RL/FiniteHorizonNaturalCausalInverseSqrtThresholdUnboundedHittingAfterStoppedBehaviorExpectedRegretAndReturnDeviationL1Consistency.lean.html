4 /-!
5 # Policy-value semantics at the genuine uncapped hittingAfter prefix
6 
7 This module evaluates the pathwise average successor-policy value gap and the
8 normalized return deviation at the same genuine uncapped `hittingAfter` prefix
9 as the accepted stopped realized-regret process. A deterministic `2H` envelope
10 and almost-everywhere random-prefix composition give behavior expected-regret
11 `L1` consistency. The exact realized/behavior/return decomposition then gives
12 return-deviation `L1` consistency.
13 
14 No expectation is commuted through the random index. This is not optional
15 stopping or a finite-index policy-value identity.
16 -/
