6 # IID finite-horizon stochastic sampled-return concentration
7 
8 This module takes finite products of complete reward-bearing trajectories.
9 Independence is only asserted across episodes. Each episode deviation remains
10 centered by the policy value at that trajectory's own sampled initial state.
11 -/
