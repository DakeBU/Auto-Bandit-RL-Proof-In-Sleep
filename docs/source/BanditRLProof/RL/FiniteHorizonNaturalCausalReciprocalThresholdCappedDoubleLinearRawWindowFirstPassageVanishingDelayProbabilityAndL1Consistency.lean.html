4 /-!
5 # Reciprocal-threshold capped first-passage delay probability and L1 consistency
6 
7 At schedule index `n`, use threshold `1/(n+1)` in the compiled capped
8 first-passage scan from `(n+1)^4` through `(n+1)^4+(2*n+1)`.
9 
10 The event that the scan does not stop at its base is exactly the one-sided
11 base-prefix threshold violation. It is therefore contained in the compiled
12 absolute-distance event. The explicit scheduled L1 envelope and Markov's
13 inequality bound its probability by an inverse-square plus inverse-linear
14 rate, which tends to zero. The compiled capped first-passage theorem supplies
15 the full stopped L1, in-measure, and almost-everywhere terminal.
16 
17 This is a moving-threshold first-moment result. It proves neither summability
18 of delay probabilities nor an uncapped hitting-time theorem.
19 -/
