3 /-!
4 # Expected consistency of stopped realized regret and policy value
5 
6 This module identifies the expected gap between stopped average realized
7 behavior regret and its successor-policy value-gap coordinate with the
8 negative expected return deviation. It proves that gap vanishes for both the
9 capped first-passage approximation and the genuine uncapped `hittingAfter`
10 prefix, then combines those vertical limits with the accepted horizontal
11 truncation limits. It does not exchange expectation with a stopping index.
12 -/
