6 # Explicit logarithmic natural-causal cumulative behavior regret
7 
8 This module closes the symbolic finite-prefix envelope for the actual
9 `source.successorPolicyAt` behavior on the one genuine heterogeneous dependent
10 causal trajectory measure. The expanded coordinate rate has an inverse-square
11 term, the scheduled exploration harmonic term, and a confidence term with
12 exponent `mdp.horizon + 5`.
13 
14 The inverse-square budget telescopes after comparison with
15 `1 / ((t + 1) * (t + 2))`, and the high-power budget is bounded by that square
16 budget. Mathlib's `harmonic_le_one_add_log` controls the remaining shifted
17 harmonic sum. The resulting expected cumulative regret is bounded by an
18 explicit coefficient times `1 + log rounds`; dividing by `rounds` gives an
19 explicit average envelope tending to zero through
20 `Real.isLittleO_log_id_atTop`.
21 
22 Regularity: the scalar sum and asymptotic lemmas are unconditional. The
23 same-source regret consumers retain finite nonempty Standard Borel
24 State/Action, a probability initial law, positive horizon/base floor/reward
25 proxy, bounded deterministic means, uniform mean-compatible selected-reward
26 sub-Gaussianity, and exploratory path support.
27 
28 Failure policy: preserve the actual exploratory behavior rather than the
29 recommended policy, the same dependent source and sampled batches, natural
30 coordinate `t` selecting successor batch `t + 1`, scheduled budgets, initial
31 batch exclusion, and both integrated model-confidence shares. This is an
32 explicit expected cumulative and average rate, not realized-return control,
33 an anytime/pathwise theorem, state reachability, a minimax-optimal rate, or
34 complete UCB-VI.
35 -/

41 theorem sum_range_one_div_natCast_add_two_sq_le_one (rounds : Nat) :
42     (Finset.range rounds).sum (fun t =>
43       1 / (((t + 2 : Nat) : Real) ^ 2)) <= 1 := by
44   calc
45     (Finset.range rounds).sum (fun t =>
46         1 / (((t + 2 : Nat) : Real) ^ 2)) <=
47         (Finset.range rounds).sum (fun t =>
48           1 / (((t + 1 : Nat) : Real) * ((t + 2 : Nat) : Real))) := by
49       apply Finset.sum_le_sum
50       intro t ht
51       apply one_div_le_one_div_of_le (by positivity)
52       push_cast
53       nlinarith [show (0 : Real) <= t by positivity]

55           1 / (((t + 1 : Nat) : Real)) -
56             1 / (((t + 2 : Nat) : Real))) := by
57       apply Finset.sum_congr rfl
58       intro t ht
59       field_simp
60       push_cast
61       ring

64         (Finset.sum_range_sub'
65           (fun t : Nat => 1 / (((t + 1 : Nat) : Real))) rounds)
66     _ <= 1 := sub_le_self _ (by positivity)
