3 /-!
4 # Simultaneous high-probability stopped-return optimality
5 
6 This module combines the six literal capped/uncapped stopped-return
7 convergence-in-measure endpoints into one measurable violation event at a
8 common schedule index. Its probability tends to zero, so every positive
9 accuracy and confidence budget is eventually satisfied simultaneously.
10 
11 This is a qualitative finite-union consequence. It assumes no independence,
12 introduces no convergence rate, and does not use optional stopping.
13 -/
