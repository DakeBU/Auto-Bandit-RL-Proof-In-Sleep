5 # Heterogeneous causal source for the actual-sampled self-consistent schedule
6 
7 The compiled self-consistent finite-window theorems vary the batch size and
8 algorithm parameters with the outer window index. Their laws therefore are not
9 prefixes of one fixed adaptive source. This module defines the distinct causal
10 algorithm in which those parameters vary at each trajectory coordinate.
11 
12 The coordinate type itself is dependent: coordinate `n` contains a stochastic
13 episode batch of size `episodes n`. Mathlib's dependent `Kernel.trajMeasure`
14 then constructs one Ionescu-Tulcea law. The terminal theorem records the exact
15 initial law, every selected next-batch conditional law, the prefix/next
16 `compProd` factorization, and projective consistency of all finite marginals.
17 
18 Local APIs/imports are the actual-sampled measurable optimistic table,
19 `exploratoryIIDStochasticEpisodeBatchKernel`, dependent
20 `Kernel.trajMeasure`, `Kernel.condDistrib_trajMeasure`,
21 `map_frestrictLe_trajMeasure_compProd_eq_map_trajMeasure`, and measurable
22 finite restrictions. Retrieval found no existing heterogeneous actual-sampled
23 causal-source route; `KernelTrajectoryPrefix.trajMeasure_map_frestrictLe_congr`
24 is supporting local evidence.
25 
26 Regularity for the law construction is finite measurable nonempty State and
27 Action with equality and measurable singletons plus a probability initial law.
28 Standard Borel State/Action is needed only for the `condDistrib` endpoint.
29 
30 Failure policy: this is a new round-varying algorithm. It preserves actual
31 sampled rewards and uses only the latest completed batch, but it does not have
32 the old fixed-window laws as marginals and does not inherit their confidence,
33 optimism, regret, or rate theorem. Those require new heterogeneous
34 concentration and regret transports. No pathwise, almost-sure, anytime,
35 minimax, reachability, or complete-UCB-VI claim is made here.
36 -/
