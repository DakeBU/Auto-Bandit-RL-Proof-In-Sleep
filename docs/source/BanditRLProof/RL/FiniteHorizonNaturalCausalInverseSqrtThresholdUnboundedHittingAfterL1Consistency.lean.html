7 # L1 consistency at the uncapped inverse-sqrt hitting time
8 
9 The positive part of the stopped average is already controlled by the hit
10 threshold.  For a delayed finite hit, first-hit minimality makes the previous
11 average positive, so the negative overshoot can only come from the final
12 successor-batch realized-regret coordinate divided by the hit index.  A
13 square-summable reciprocal weight and uniform coordinate L2 control then make
14 that overshoot vanish.  This is not optional stopping.
