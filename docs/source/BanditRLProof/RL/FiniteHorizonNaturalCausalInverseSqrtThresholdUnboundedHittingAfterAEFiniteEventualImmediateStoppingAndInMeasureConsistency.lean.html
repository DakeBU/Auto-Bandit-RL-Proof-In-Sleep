21 /-!
22 This route replaces the finite `hittingBtwn` scan by Mathlib's genuine
23 uncapped `hittingAfter`. All-prefix almost-sure convergence proves that every
24 fixed schedule-indexed hitting time is finite almost surely. The compiled
25 summable-delay route separately proves that almost every trajectory eventually
26 hits immediately at the fourth-power base. These facts yield a diverging
27 stopped subsequence and hence stopped-process convergence almost everywhere
28 and in measure. No expected-delay, uniform-integrability, L1, or optional-
29 stopping claim is made.
30 -/
