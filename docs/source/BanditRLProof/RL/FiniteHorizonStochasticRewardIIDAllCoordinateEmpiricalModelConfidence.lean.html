3 /-!
4 # All-coordinate iid empirical-model confidence with stochastic rewards
5 
6 This module combines the compiled sampled-reward coordinate tail with the
7 existing simultaneous count/transition event.  It keeps the complete
8 reward-bearing iid trajectory family as the probability space and constructs
9 the empirical model from the actual sampled rewards.
