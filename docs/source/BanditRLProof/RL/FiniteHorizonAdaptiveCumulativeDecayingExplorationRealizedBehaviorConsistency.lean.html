3 /-!
4 # Decaying-exploration realized behavior consistency
5 
6 This module closes the scalar asymptotic boundary left by the finite-window
7 realized-regret transport.  The coarse whole-batch return proxy simplifies
8 exactly, so the scheduled normalized return radius is bounded by
9 `2 * horizon / (n + 2)` and tends to zero independently of the scheduled batch
10 size.  Adding this radius to the compiled exploratory-behavior expected-regret
11 certificate yields a realized certificate tending to zero, while the union of
12 the count and return events has a two-share failure budget tending to zero.
13 
14 The terminal theorem is a dependent family of finite-window certificates.  Its
15 sample space and trajectory measure may change with `n`; it does not claim one
16 common process, convergence in probability, an almost-sure result, or an
17 anytime theorem.
18 -/

56 /-- The successor-return proxy contains exactly `rounds` nonzero batch terms. -/
57 theorem cumulativeSuccessorReturnVarianceProxy_coe
58     (mdp : MDP State Action) (episodes rounds : Nat) :
59     ((cumulativeSuccessorReturnVarianceProxy mdp episodes rounds : NNReal) : Real) =
60       (rounds : Real) *
61         ((episodes : Real) * (mdp.horizon : Real)) ^ 2 := by
62   rw [cumulativeSuccessorReturnVarianceProxy]
63   induction rounds with
64   | zero => simp
65   | succ rounds ih =>
66       rw [Nat.cast_succ]
67       rw [Finset.sum_range_succ, NNReal.coe_add, ih]
68       simp only [MarkovPolicy.batchReturnVarianceProxy,
69         Concentration.intervalVarianceProxy, NNReal.coe_pow, NNReal.coe_div,
70         coe_nnnorm]
71       norm_num
72       rw [abs_of_nonneg (by positivity)]
73       ring
