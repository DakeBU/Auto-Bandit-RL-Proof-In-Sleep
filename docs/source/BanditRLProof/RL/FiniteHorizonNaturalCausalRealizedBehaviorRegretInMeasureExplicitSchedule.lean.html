3 /-!
4 # Explicit polynomial-prefix absolute consistency in measure
5 
6 This module upgrades the one-sided scheduled certificate to Mathlib
7 `TendstoInMeasure` for the same equal-round-weighted natural realized
8 behavior-regret process. Outside the compiled model-tail/return event, the
9 parent route supplies the upper bound. The exact expected-minus-deviation
10 identity, expected-regret nonnegativity, and the return-event complement
11 supply the missing lower bound.
12 
13 The process differs from the existing mass-weighted
14 `realizedSuccessorAverageRegret` process. The result is only for the explicit
15 fourth-power prefix subsequence; it is not all-prefix, anytime, almost-sure,
16 or L1 convergence.
17 -/
