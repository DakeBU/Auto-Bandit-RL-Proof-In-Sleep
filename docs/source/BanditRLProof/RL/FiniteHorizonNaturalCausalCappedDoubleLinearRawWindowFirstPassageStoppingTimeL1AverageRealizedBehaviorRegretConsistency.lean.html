4 /-!
5 # Capped double-linear raw-window first-passage stopping-time L1 consistency
6 
7 At schedule index `n`, this module scans the exact natural average realized
8 behavior-regret process from the fourth-power prefix `(n+1)^4` through the
9 right endpoint `(n+1)^4+(2*n+1)`. It stops at the first prefix where the
10 process is at most a deterministic threshold, or at the right endpoint if no
11 earlier prefix crosses the threshold.
12 
13 Mathlib's `hittingBtwn` supplies the finite first-passage construction and its
14 stopping-time theorem. The compiled rate-controlled raw-window parent then
15 gives the full L1, in-measure, and almost-everywhere consistency package. No
16 optional-stopping identity or independence assumption is used.
17 -/
