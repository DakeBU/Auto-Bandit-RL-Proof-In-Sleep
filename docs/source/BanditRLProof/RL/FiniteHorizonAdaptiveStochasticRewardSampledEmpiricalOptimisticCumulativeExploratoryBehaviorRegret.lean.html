5 # Cumulative successor exploratory-behavior regret for sampled empirical optimism
6 
7 This module charges uniform exploration around the actual sampled-model
8 recommendations.  The policy built from coordinate `round` is the adaptive
9 source policy for successor batch `round + 1`; the initial policy and realized
10 sampled returns remain outside this theorem route.
11 -/

117     (rewardBound : Real)
