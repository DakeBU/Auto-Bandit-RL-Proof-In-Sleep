6 /-!
7 # Concrete stochastic empirical-optimistic realized behavior regret
8 
9 This module combines the concrete stochastic known-mean empirical-transition
10 source with the global sampled-return transport.  The count/optimism event and
11 the sampled-return event retain separate confidence budgets.  The expected
12 regret of the exploratory behavior is charged explicitly against the projected
13 recommended policy before the realized-return deviation is added.
14 
15 The result remains a fixed-window theorem for successor batches.  It does not
16 estimate stochastic reward means, include the initial batch in realized regret,
17 or claim an anytime, minimax, or complete UCB-VI result.
18 -/

82             expectedBound +
83               Concentration.subGaussianSumConfidenceRadius
84                   (cumulativeSuccessorGlobalReturnVarianceProxy

86                   returnDelta /
87                 ((episodes : Real) * (rounds : Real)) := by

89     rounds rewardBound rewardVarianceProxy returnDelta
