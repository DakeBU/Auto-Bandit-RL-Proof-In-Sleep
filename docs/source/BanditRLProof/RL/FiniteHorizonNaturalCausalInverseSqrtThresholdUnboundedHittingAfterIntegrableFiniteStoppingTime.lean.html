9 /-!
10 # First moment of the uncapped inverse-sqrt hitting time
11 
12 This module upgrades each fixed-index genuine `hittingAfter` first passage
13 from almost-sure finiteness to first-moment integrability.  The proof uses the
14 compiled fourth-power burn-in checkpoints.  A cubic block-width envelope is
15 summable against both the infinite model-tail budget and the exponentially
16 small return share.  Once the deterministic checkpoint regret rate lies below
17 the fixed positive threshold, delayed checkpoints are contained in the
18 compiled violation events.
19 
20 This is a first-moment result.  It does not prove a second moment, uniform
21 integrability of stopped rewards, L1 convergence of the stopped process, or an
22 optional-stopping identity.
23 -/
