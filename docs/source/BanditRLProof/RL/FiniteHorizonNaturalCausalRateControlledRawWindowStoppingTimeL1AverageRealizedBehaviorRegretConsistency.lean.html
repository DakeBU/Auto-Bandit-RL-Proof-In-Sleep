3 /-!
4 # Rate-controlled raw-window stopping-time L1 consistency
5 
6 This module replaces the fixed fourth-power base and width `n` by deterministic
7 functions `baseRounds` and `windowWidth`. The exact regularity contract is
8 
9 `(windowWidth n + 1) / sqrt (baseRounds n) -> 0`.
10 
11 Every stopping prefix may select any raw natural coordinate in
12 `[baseRounds n, baseRounds n + windowWidth n]`. The proof charges the selector
13 to the finite sum of all candidate-coordinate L1 envelopes. The extra contract
14 `n <= baseRounds n` is used only by the almost-sure parent theorem; no optional
15 stopping identity or independence assumption is introduced.
16 -/
