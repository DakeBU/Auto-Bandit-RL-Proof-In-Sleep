4 /-!
5 # Realized successor regret for actual sampled empirical optimism
6 
7 This module combines the actual sampled-model confidence and exploratory
8 successor-policy route with the existing globally centered stochastic return
9 tail.  Model count, model reward, and realized-return failures retain three
10 separate confidence shares.  The result covers successor batches `1..rounds`;
11 the initial batch and explicit occupancy-radius rates remain downstream.
12 -/
