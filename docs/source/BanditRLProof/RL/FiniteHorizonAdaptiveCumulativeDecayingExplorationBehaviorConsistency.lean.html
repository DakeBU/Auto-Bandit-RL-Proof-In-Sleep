4 # Decaying-exploration high-probability behavior consistency
5 
6 This module closes the fixed-exploration residual charge in the exploratory
7 behavior-regret route.  It starts from one path-support visit floor at full
8 exploration, proves the exact stagewise power scaling at a smaller exploration
9 rate, and chooses
10 
11 * `explorationRate_n = 1 / (n + 2)`,
12 * `rounds_n = (n + 2) ^ (horizon + 4)`, and
13 * `visitFloor_n = baseVisitFloor * explorationRate_n ^ horizon`.
14 
15 The effective visit mass is therefore `baseVisitFloor * (n + 2) ^ 4`.
16 The scheduled recommendation certificate is `O((n + 2) ^ -2)`, while the
17 behavior exploration charge is `O((n + 2) ^ -1)`.  Both the confidence budget
18 and the resulting deterministic average exploratory-behavior expected-regret
19 certificate tend to zero.
20 
21 Each finite window still has its own episode count and trajectory type.  The
22 source theorem is consequently a family of finite-window outer-measure
23 certificates, not a pathwise, almost-sure, realized-regret, or common-process
24 convergence theorem.
25 -/
