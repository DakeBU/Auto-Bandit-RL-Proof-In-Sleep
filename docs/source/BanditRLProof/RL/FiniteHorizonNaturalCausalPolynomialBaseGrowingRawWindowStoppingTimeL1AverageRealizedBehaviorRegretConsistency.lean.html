5 /-!
6 # Polynomial-base growing raw-window stopping-time L1 consistency
7 
8 This module strengthens the fourth-power grid stopping theorem to a contiguous
9 window of raw natural prefixes. At schedule index `n`, the stopping prefix may
10 select any integer in `[(n + 1)^4, (n + 1)^4 + n]`. The all-prefix L1 envelope
11 is bounded by one inverse square root, so every candidate costs at most
12 `D / (n + 1)^2`; summing the `n + 1` raw candidates gives `D / (n + 1)`.
13 
14 The base points remain the explicit fourth-power schedule. This does not cover
15 arbitrary raw base indices, wider windows, optional stopping, or independence.
16 -/
