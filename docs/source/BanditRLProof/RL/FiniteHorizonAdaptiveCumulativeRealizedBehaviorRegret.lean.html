4 # Adaptive finite-window realized behavior regret
5 
6 This module transports the compiled adaptive exploratory-policy expected-regret
7 route to realized rewards on the same infinite episode-batch trajectory law.
8 Successor coordinates `1` through `rounds` are charged; coordinate zero is the
9 uncontrolled initial batch and is excluded.
10 
11 Each batch return is centered by the exact history-kernel integral.  The
12 bounded-reward contract gives the coarse whole-batch Hoeffding proxy
13 `(episodes * horizon)^2`; this is sufficient for a finite-window realized
14 average certificate, but it is not the sharper within-batch episode proxy.
15 The final decaying-exploration theorem unions the existing cumulative-count bad
16 event with the return-deviation bad event, so its failure budget is two copies
17 of the scheduled confidence level.  No common probability space across
18 schedule indices, anytime statement, almost-sure result, or minimax rate is
19 claimed here.
20 -/
