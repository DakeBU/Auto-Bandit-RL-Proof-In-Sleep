4 /-!
5 # Fixed-prefix high-probability natural causal realized behavior regret
6 
7 This module converts the compiled fixed-prefix logarithmic behavior-expected
8 regret route into a realized route on the same heterogeneous dependent causal
9 source.  Natural round `t` uses the sample average of the actual successor
10 batch at coordinate `t + 1`, generated by the exploratory policy selected from
11 the prefix through `t`.
12 
13 The return process keeps coordinate zero equal to zero and divides each
14 globally centered successor-batch return by its own positive batch size.
15 Mathlib's scalar sub-Gaussian MGF transport therefore multiplies the existing
16 conditional proxy by the square of that reciprocal.  The existing
17 strongly-adapted finite-sum theorem gives a fixed-prefix two-sided return tail.
18 
19 Regularity: finite nonempty measurable State/Action with measurable
20 singletons, a probability initial law, Standard Borel source spaces, positive
21 round count and scheduled batch sizes, bounded stored means, a uniform
22 selected-reward sub-Gaussian law, positive horizon/proxy/base floor, and
23 exploratory path support.
24 
25 Failure policy: preserve the actual causal source, successor indexing, global
26 initial-law centering, schedule-specific batch sizes, and separate model and
27 return failure shares.  This is fixed-prefix control of batch-average realized
28 behavior regret.  It is not arbitrary-delta model control, a one-raw-episode
29 online process, uniform-time/anytime control, minimax/optimal-rate regret, or a
30 complete UCB-VI theorem.
31 -/
