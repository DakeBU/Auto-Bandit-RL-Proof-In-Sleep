5 /-!
6 # History-adaptive corrupted finite-arm IID reward laws
7 
8 The base reward vector is fresh IID at every round.  Before the current action
9 is sampled, a measurable arm-dependent reward shift may be selected from the
10 preceding finite pair history.  Shifted rewards are clipped to `[0, 1]`.  A
11 deterministic time-and-arm envelope controls the resulting baseline-gap
12 perturbation and therefore supplies the additive regret budget.
13 -/
