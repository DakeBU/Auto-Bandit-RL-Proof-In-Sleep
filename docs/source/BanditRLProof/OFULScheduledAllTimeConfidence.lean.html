4 /-!
5 # Scheduled canonical all-time OFUL confidence
6 
7 This module defines one history algorithm whose successor selector at history
8 index `n` uses the confidence budget assigned to horizon `n + 1`.  It then
9 constructs the corresponding strict-past predictable feature and residual
10 process and transports the all-time scalar-ridge confidence event to the
11 actual canonical trajectory.
12 
13 The final theorem also controls the event that any successor action violates
14 its scheduled one-step optimism certificate.  A concrete environment-to-
15 residual-law producer remains a separate law-transport theorem.
16 -/
