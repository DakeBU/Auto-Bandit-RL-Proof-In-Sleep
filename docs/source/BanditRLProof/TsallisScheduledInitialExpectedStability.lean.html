3 /-!
4 # Expected scheduled half-Tsallis initial stability
5 
6 This module closes the time-zero conditioning surface omitted from the
7 scheduled successor stability theorem.  The left side is the actual time-zero
8 conjugate-potential term from the scheduled pathwise decomposition.
9 
10 The local rate must satisfy `0 < eta 0 <= 1 / 2`.  Rates above `1 / 2` remain a
11 separate fallback leaf.
12 -/
