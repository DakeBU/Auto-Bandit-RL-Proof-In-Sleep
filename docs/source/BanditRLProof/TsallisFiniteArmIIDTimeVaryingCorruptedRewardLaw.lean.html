3 /-!
4 # Time-varying corrupted finite-arm IID reward laws
5 
6 The base reward vector is fresh IID at every round.  Before play begins, a
7 deterministic arm-dependent reward shift is fixed for every round; shifted
8 rewards are clipped back to `[0,1]`.  Such a schedule is predictable and
9 oblivious.  It need not be stationary, but it does not depend on the realized
10 history.
11 -/
