4 /-!
5 # Expected regret for generated scheduled half-Tsallis FTRL
6 
7 This module transports the observed importance-weighted regret of the
8 scheduled half-Tsallis trajectory to predictable environment regret.  It then
9 combines the pathwise time-varying penalty theorem with the all-rate expected
10 stability theorem.
