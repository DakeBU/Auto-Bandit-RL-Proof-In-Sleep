5 # Predictable EXP3 regret with random predictable mixed-square variance
6 
7 This module transports the fixed-horizon predictable-variance tail to the
8 observed mixed estimator-square sum used by the sampled Hedge inequality.  It
9 then exposes a predictable-regret theorem whose only uncontrolled probability
10 is the overflow event for the cumulative predictable variance.
