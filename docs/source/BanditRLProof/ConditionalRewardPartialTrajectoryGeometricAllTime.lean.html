4 /-!
5 # Generated finite-arm empirical-mean geometric all-time confidence
6 
7 This module instantiates the canonical action/reward trajectory's fixed-arm,
8 fixed-successor-horizon random-pull-count empirical-mean tail at one geometric
9 confidence share per time and arm. The compiled finite-index geometric
10 all-time adapter then controls the union over every positive successor horizon
11 and every arm in a nonempty finite action type.
12 
13 The result is a model-specific confidence producer for the canonical generated
14 trajectory. It is not a UCB policy, pull-count regret argument, maximal
15 inequality, optional-stopping theorem, self-normalized bound, or general
16 Freedman inequality.
17 -/
