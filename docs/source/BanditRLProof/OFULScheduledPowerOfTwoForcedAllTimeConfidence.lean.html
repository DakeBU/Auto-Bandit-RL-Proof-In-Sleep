5 # All-time confidence for power-of-two forced telescoping OFUL
6 
7 This module specializes the generic deterministic-history-selector confidence
8 machinery to the horizon-independent power-of-two forced policy. Its terminal
9 all-horizon pseudo-regret tail keeps the deterministic forced-action charge
10 explicit; a logarithmic scalar bound for that charge is a separate downstream
11 obligation.
