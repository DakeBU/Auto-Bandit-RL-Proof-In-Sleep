21 /--
22 The lower integral of a finite weighted sum of scalar-casted pull counts equals
23 the same finite weighted sum of the corresponding action-event measures.
24 
25 This is the `EXP-WEIGHTED-PULLCOUNT-LINTEGRAL` bridge.  It is shaped like a
26 nonnegative expected-regret identity, but it deliberately avoids
27 `FiniteBanditModel`, `Rat`, `Real`, Bochner expectation, filtrations, kernels,
28 and concentration assumptions.
29 -/
