10 # Horizon-local history-adaptive expected corruption
11 
12 This module packages only the predictable reward-shift data used through a
13 fixed finite horizon.  The source is extended by zero after that horizon and
14 then consumed by the existing all-time expected-corruption theorem route.
15 -/

17 /-- Predictable reward shifts and deterministic envelope witnesses needed only
18 for rounds `0, ..., horizon`.  Successor data are indexed by `Fin horizon`, so
19 no measurability or boundedness contract is requested after the final round. -/
