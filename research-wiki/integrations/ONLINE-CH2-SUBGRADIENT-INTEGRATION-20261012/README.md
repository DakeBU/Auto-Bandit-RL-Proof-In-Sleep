## Source and implementation boundary

Closedness and properness retain literal source formulas over generalized carriers. The support predicate extends the printed proper-function carrier to all extended-real functions. The differentiability predicate specifies an ambient real differentiable representative that agrees with f throughout a neighborhood. These are four Lean definitions, not four literal source definitions.

G-T22-SCOPE remains open: the source defines subgradients for proper functions but prints a wider carrier in Theorem2.22 without reconciling it. G-T22-DIFF remains open: the inspected passage does not define extended-valued differentiability. The library interpretation proves the equivalence without adding properness or interior as terminal hypotheses; both directions derive the needed no-bottom and interior facts. This is a precise library/source-adapter result, not a claim that the source supplied those definitions or that its ambiguities have been repaired.

The support-point lemma drops convexity from the source observation, a stronger result. Theorem2.21 preserves globally real f, convex V and an existing global support at each point of V; one support at the convex mixture supplies both weighted inequalities. Arbitrary real inner-product spaces generalize the Euclidean source and need neither completeness nor finite dimension for this result.

Interior existence uses finite-dimensional geometry and canonical affine contact. The relative-interior helper works in a real normed space: restrict to the original domain affine span, preserve contact at the specified point, extend the functional and correct its intercept. Riesz gives global vectors in the inner-product version. No contact or support oracle is supplied.

The differentiability proof uses a real germ for forward gradient support and uniqueness. In reverse, a nonzero boundary normal would produce a second support, so singleton support forces ambient interior. Local convex Lipschitzness, nonbottom-filter limits and compactness give convergence of nearby supports. Two global inequalities then squeeze the finite residual to a Frechet derivative. Proof-internal choice is not an algorithm or measurable selection. Smoothness of total toReal alone is insufficient, as the real singleton-indicator diagnostics demonstrate.

The source gives only external proof citations for several anchors. The displayed proof bridges are library explanations, not invented author proofs. Theorem2.22 cites Rockafellar Theorem25.1; the distinct Theorem2.7 citation pair is not substituted for it. Chapter2, the whole book, purification and full Exposition Seal remain incomplete.
