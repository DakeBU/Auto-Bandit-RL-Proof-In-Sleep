# Bounded actual FTL obstruction and exact converse

For a deterministic observation stream in [0,1], squared-loss FTL starts at 1/2 and thereafter predicts the mean of strict past observations. Finite nonpositive normalized regret limits at every fixed unit comparator exist exactly when the empirical means converge. The converse uses comparator endpoints 0 and 1 to reconstruct the empirical mean from its two squared distances.

One literal dyadic stream starts at zero and sets d_(n+1)=1-d_(floor(n/2)). It stays in [0,1]. Pairing its equal children gives exact prefix counts. Its empirical means tend to 2/3 along horizons 4^(n+1)-1 and to 1/3 along horizons 2*4^n-1. Both horizons are cofinal. The same actual FTL predictor has upper-epsilon no-regret and normalized interval-best regret tending to zero, but normalized signed regret against comparator zero has no finite ordinary limit. Any such limit would force the squared empirical means to tend to both 4/9 and 1/9. This also refutes the all-comparator ordinary-limit predicate for this process. The obstruction proof does not depend on the converse theorem.
