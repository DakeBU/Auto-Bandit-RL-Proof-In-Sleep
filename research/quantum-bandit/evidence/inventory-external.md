**QuAIR shared foundations.** [Base/POVMProbability.lean](https://github.com/QuAIR/Lean-QIT-Bench/blob/231830d5ab1624557fb88cec328c0c3e9ebcae52/QITBench/Base/POVMProbability.lean) defines `QITBench.POVM.prob : ℝ≥0` through the diagonal of the measured state. `sum_prob` proves sum one; `prob_le_one` proves an outcome probability at most one; `prob_eq_trace_re` identifies it with `Re Tr(rho.matrix * M.effects outcome)`. ## Bandit local reuse: actual premises and conclusions

The freeze JSON records all 31 files inspected: both requested generic leaves and every `*HOO*.lean` under `BanditRLProof`. No active placeholder tokens were found in these files. Existing production sources were read only; no local compilation was attempted in this audit.

`BanditRLProof.realMeanRegret_eq_sum_gap_mul_pullCount` needs only `mean : Fin K → ℝ`, an action trace, and a finite horizon. It proves the exact deterministic equality between supremum-minus-mean pseudo-regret and gap-weighted pull counts. `integral_realMeanRegret_eq_sum_gap_mul_integral_pullCount` additionally requires a measurable sample space, a measure, and integrability of each real-valued pull count. It requires no reward-law, sub-Gaussian or policy premise. This is directly useful **after** the application defines what each action/pull represents; it does not equate a quantum query with a pull.

`BanditRLProof.Concentration` defines geometric shares `δ/(2·2^n)` and telescoping shares `δ/((n+1)(n+2))`. `tsum_ofReal_geometricConfidenceShare` and `tsum_ofReal_telescopingConfidenceShare` require only `0 ≤ δ` and prove exact ENNReal budget sums. Positive-share lemmas require `0 < δ`. These arithmetic leaves neither construct a confidence event nor prove its failure probability; quantum estimates can consume the budgets once their own per-stage probability bounds exist.

The HOO series has a substantive causal process, not only a rate wrapper. `HOOTree/HOOHistory/HOOMeasurable/HOOPrefix/HOOTrajectory` construct tree selection, reward histories, causal and measurable actions, and an infinite reward trajectory from a **Markov reward kernel**, with the next reward conditioned on the observed prefix. `HOOModel` supplies actual arm representatives and countable node laws. `RegularCovering` contains root/children/nonempty regions, measurable regions, nonnegative dissimilarity vanishing on the diagonal, positive `ν₁,ν₂`, `0<ρ<1`, shrinking diameter, contained inner balls and same-depth disjoint balls. It does not assume a triangle inequality.

`BanditRLProof.HOO.bounded_node_subgaussian` in `HOOConditionalMGF.lean` requires an IsMarkovKernel and each node reward's almost-sure support in `[0,1]`; its centered reward has variance proxy `1/4`. `region_step_fixedMGF` gives log-MGF budget `tilt²/8` only when a region is selected. `trajectory_region_condMGF` derives the corresponding conditional bound for the **constructed trajectory**, with predictable selection and exponential integrability proved. `region_noise_count_tail` in `HOOConcentration.lean` then bounds the event `threshold ≤ sum regional noise` and `sum regional counts ≤ countBudget` by `exp(-tilt·threshold + tilt²·countBudget/8)`, for nonnegative tilt. This is not a free confidence premise.

`HOOConfidence.region_deviation_confidence` assumes those bounded Markov laws and `L≥0`, and proves the probability of positive visits and regional deviation at least `sqrt(2·visits·L)` is at most `n exp(-4L)`. `HOOIndexConfidence` combines this with descendant means/geometric optimism. `HOOPathComparison/HOOSelectionTail` turn sufficiently many visits to a genuinely poor region into a finite union of confidence failures. `HOOExpectedVisits.poor_region_lintegral_visits` additionally needs exact kernel means, a global supremum `best`, weak Lipschitzness, and `ν₁ρ^depth < best-regionSup`; it proves expected visits at most `visitThreshold(effective gap,N)+3`. `HOOTailSum` is the deterministic summability leaf behind that `+3`.

`HOOGeometry/HOOLevels/HOOOptimalBranch/HOOPacking/HOODimension/HOOPartition` provide geometric and near-optimality packing arguments. `HOORegretPartition/HOOExpectedRegret/HOORegretAlgebra/HOODepthOptimization` partition regret, bound expected bad visits, sum depth contributions and choose a depth. Their algebraic leaves may be useful after a new model supplies the full relevant confidence and counting premises.

`RegularCovering.expected_pseudoRegret_rate` requires the above covering, an IsMarkovKernel on arms, **exact** mean equalities, `f(x)≤best`, `f(x)∈[0,1]`, `regionSup f univ=best`, weak Lipschitzness, bounded reward support, and actual near-optimality dimension strictly below real `d`. It concludes one positive constant `γ`, independent of horizon, bounding expected pseudo-regret at every `N≥1` by

`γ N^((d+1)/(d+2)) log(max(N,2))^(1/(d+2))`.

`HOOActualRegret.expected_actual_eq_pseudoRegret` uses exact reward means and bounded support to identify expected realized and pseudo-regret for this same causal trajectory. `expected_actualRegret_rate` adds the geometric/rate premises. `HOORewardFamily` removes measurability of the entire arm-to-law family by a discrete-domain transport while retaining probability laws, exact means, bounded support and the same process; it does not remove those stochastic obligations. `HOOCantorModel/HOOCantorRate` instantiate a particular countably represented Cantor-arm model with bounded non-Dirac rewards; they do not establish a generic quantum-oracle model.

## Unsafe reuse and precise missing leaves

A quantum estimator guarantee `Pr(|estimate-mean|>ε)≤δ` does **not** imply the centered bounded-reward conditional MGF that HOO constructs. Estimates can be biased; their errors may depend on prior choices, share quantum state, be non-independent, or charge many oracle/inverse-oracle calls per update. Even clipping an estimate to `[0,1]` establishes support only, not its conditional mean. Feeding estimates into the existing reward trajectory changes both the measured process and the realized-regret meaning. The HOO stochastic chain must not be reused by relabeling estimator errors as rewards.

The safe reusable boundary is: licensed quantum oracle/state semantics → compiled estimation/testing primitive → explicit predictable classical confidence contract with failure budgets and query cost → decision/counting layer → regret. Deterministic gap/pull bookkeeping and confidence-share sums are immediately useful candidate consumers; the HOO reward-MGF chain is a separate producer with stronger hypotheses.

Missing compiled leaves are: (1) canonical finite state/unitary/measurement adapters to destination quantum semantics, including basis/tensor/chronological ordering; (2) Born normalization and any required operator-norm perturbation-to-outcome-probability bound in the destination representation; (3) coherent reward-oracle and inverse-access semantics with query accounting; (4) a quantum mean-estimation/testing confidence theorem, including adaptive conditional use and estimator bias; (5) a confidence-only decision interface if the existing bounded-reward producer does not apply; (6) query-versus-pull/regret transport; (7) declaration-level transitive axiom checks, local `lake build && lake build Tests`, and the protocols' source/semantic/publication gates for any promoted adapter.

No such adapter or quantum confidence theorem was proved or compiled by this audit. Concurrent parent/worker implementation files are outside this frozen-source inventory and require their own build evidence. This inventory contributes source seals, candidate reuse routes and explicit obstructions; it does not advance the certified quantum-bandit theorem frontier.
