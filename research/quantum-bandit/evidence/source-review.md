## Binder/definition and topology audit

The high-risk anchor inputs are correctly restricted: finite carriers (TYPING), normalized input, two actual unitary oracles/matrices, Loewner effect bounds and operator distance (SOURCE for the sealed Born/query perturbation statements). Word-level unitarity, output normalization, effect contraction, evaluated-word distance, basis mass normalization, and `q²≤Dq` are dependency results. They are not promoted to source-facing hypotheses. The aligned-circuit witness is SOURCE for the explicitly conditional aligned perturbation consumer; no rounding supplier is claimed.

Decision/confidence leaves have a distinct role. A good confidence event and statistical tails are inputs of **transport theorems**, not closure of the user-requested quantum estimator or A/B algorithm. The actual recommendation theorem removes the auxiliary maximizer certificate by proving it for its chosen recommendation. The joint adapter removes arbitrary circuit bias as a premise by producing it from `Aligned`. `hbudget` is a deliberately selected fidelity/statistical budget and `htail` is the still-open producer interface. The supplied measure is arbitrary, no probability-normalization instance is required, and estimator measurability/time-uniform confidence is not produced; the finite event bound is valid as an outer-measure inequality. A PAC interpretation requires the missing probability/estimator interface. Treating these transport inputs as unexplained premises of a published unconditional quantum-regret/PAC algorithm would violate the source protocol.

Definitions are literal list evaluators, direct finite sums/quadratic forms, interval filtering and an existing fixed-enumeration argmax. No off-source default, min-over-empty feasible set, fake producer axiom or novel state-copy/unitary identification was found. `recommend` uses the existing nonempty finite-arm argmax, making ties a specified enumeration choice. Generalizing these leaves from `K≥2` to nonempty arms is harmless but does not supply the full user's `K≥2,T,D` performance result.

The inspected analytic proof topology matches its seal: split quadratic-form difference, Cauchy–Schwarz, effect contraction, unitary normalization, then operator application. Circuit bias follows the actual existing Ry/List.Forall₂ telescoping result. Query-word distance uses telescoping with actual unitary factors and star isometry; Born/Hellinger consumers reuse it. The recommendation path is circuit bias plus a supplied statistical good event, a concrete argmax implication, event inclusion, and finite union bound. Imports are module edges; no assertion of exhaustive elaborated proof-term dependencies is made by this source review.
