Date 2026-10-09. Exploratory local mathematics; not a quantum estimation producer.
For finite nonempty arms, define intervals [estimate_i-radius_i, estimate_i+radius_i]. Keep arm i iff every lower endpoint is <= its upper endpoint (ties retained). On the explicit good event |estimate_i-mu_i|<=radius_i for every arm: (1) any true maximizer survives; (2) if all radii <=r and gap_i>4r, arm i is removed. This supplies a stage invariant, not an adaptive probability theorem. Binder conditions are SOURCE for the interval lemma, but are RULED/open dependencies for the final bandit algorithm; final regret must not assert them as unexplained hypotheses.
For epsilon BAI, select a maximal estimated mean. If each target-vs-fixed-fidelity bias <=b_i and each fidelity-vs-estimate error <=s_i with b_i+s_i<=epsilon/2, selection is epsilon optimal. There is no claim of choosing a fidelity with unknown means or of mixed-fidelity optimality. Statistical events and costed implementable estimator remain explicit open leaves.
For accounting, a block charges forward+inverse calls to its single classical arm, and a list of blocks charges sum q*gap.
