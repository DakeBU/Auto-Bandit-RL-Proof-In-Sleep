## Conventions for the seven slots

Each row supplies objects, parameter range, assumptions, quantifiers, output, costs, and claim scope. An implicit argument is universally quantified just like an explicit argument. A definition describes the function or type it introduces; it is not an existential guarantee or a procedure with proved running time. `None` in the cost slot means no complexity or resource bound occurs in that declaration.

For the matrix rows, **M-context** means a type `ι` with `Fintype ι` and `DecidableEq ι`, square complex matrices on `ι`, and complex Euclidean vectors on `ι`. `ι` is not required to be nonempty. The opened matrix norm is the Euclidean operator norm: the inspected mathlib definition identifies it with the operator norm of `Matrix.toEuclideanCLM`. Matrix order is the positive-semidefinite order: `A ≤ B` means `B − A` is positive semidefinite, so `0 ≤ P ≤ 1` is an effect condition, not entrywise inequalities. Matrix `star` is the conjugate transpose, and unitary membership means the corresponding adjoint products are identity. Write `p(U,P,ψ) = Re ⟨Uψ,P(Uψ)⟩` for the packet's `probability` definition, and `q(w)`, `f(w)`, `i(w)`, `k(w)` for its query, forward, inverse, and known counts.

For the confidence and accounting rows, `Fin K` is the finite arm set, with natural `K`; real-valued functions need not take values in `[0,1]`. Unless explicitly required, `K` may be zero. `ofReal(x)` is the extended nonnegative-real embedding of `max(x,0)`; in particular nonpositive shares contribute zero. A `Measure` here is arbitrary and need not have mass one. Its evaluation on an arbitrary set is through its outer-measure function, so absent measurability hypotheses do not make these displayed measure inequalities ill-typed; they do limit the interpretation as conventional probability statements.

## Boundaries and possible overreadings

1. **Conditional versus algorithmic content.** The perturbation theorems take approximate matrices/circuits and certificates as input. The confidence theorems take bias, statistical budgets, and tail certificates as input. `recommend` and `survivors` are exact-real noncomputable definitions. The packet does not present an implemented quantum estimator, an approximation compiler, a sequential bandit policy, or a sample/query complexity theorem.
2. **Constructive and finite-bit boundary.** Exact complex unitary matrices can occur as known gates; primitive angles can contain arbitrary reals. No finite precision representation, rounding procedure, comparison termination certificate, bit bound, gate synthesis, or precision-dependent cost is present. Existence/implementation of a circuit satisfying `Aligned` is not concluded by `aligned_reward_bias_le`.
3. **Three distinct counts.** Primitive circuit `length` counts every primitive gate. Query-word `q` counts forward and inverse markers and assigns zero to known gates. Accounting `queries` sums counters supplied in a block and synthetically replicates its arm. The packet has no formal bridge equating these three counts or extracting block counters from an evaluated word. The local bound named `D` does not inherently mean parallel depth.
4. **Inverse-call meaning.** In a word the inverse marker means the adjoint; under unitary hypotheses it is inverse access. Its cost is one by definition. In a block, inverse is only a natural counter, and inverse-only gap charging is a proved identity of the adopted formula. This does not derive an environmental notion of regret for inverse access.
5. **Probability boundary.** The Born scalar becomes a number in `[0,1]` only with normalization, unitarity, and effect hypotheses, subject to acceptance of the displayed theorem. No measurement law connects that scalar to any `ν`, `μ`, or estimator in the confidence file. Confidence failure inequalities are valid measure-form statements if accepted; probability interpretation additionally requires a probability measure and, when desired, measurable outcome functions/events. Those conditions are not asserted here.
6. **Assumption usage.** No plainly unused named premise was observed in a packet theorem's complete proof. Several premises are stronger than needed in special cases: matrix-distance premises are irrelevant for a known instruction, unitarity is irrelevant for an empty word, and block boundedness is unused by the sum identities. These are special-case redundancies, not evidence of a universally unused premise. Confidence certificates imply nonnegative radii on active arms; norm-error certificates imply nonnegative tolerances. The failure theorem permits arbitrary signed statistical allowances and shares; clipping by `ofReal` and the tail premises handle these without an explicit positivity hypothesis.
7. **Unproved bridges.** The packet gives no theorem connecting circuit/query-word scalar perturbation to a named arm's mean bias certificate, selecting a fidelity from a target confidence budget, deriving statistical tails from a sampler, splitting a total failure budget, or transporting accounting charge to an interaction-based expected regret. Such bridges cannot be inferred from shared vocabulary in declaration names.
