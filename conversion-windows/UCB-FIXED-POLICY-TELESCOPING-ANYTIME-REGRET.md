# Conversion Window: Fixed-policy telescoping anytime UCB regret

Task id: `UCB-FIXED-POLICY-TELESCOPING-ANYTIME-REGRET`

Source card: `TXT-LATTIMORE-SZEPESVARI-2020`; `TXT-BUBECK-CESABIANCHI-2012`; `PPR-AUER-CBF-2002-UCB1`
Scenario card: `SCN-STOCHASTIC-FINITE`

## Natural-Language Statement

There is one horizon-free scheduled UCB policy. At post-history round `t`, each
arm receives its generated empirical mean plus the random-count peeling radius
with confidence share `telescopingConfidenceShare delta t / K`. On the one
canonical trajectory generated by this policy, the union of every positive-
count deviation over every arm and time has outer measure at most `delta`.
Outside that event, every positive-gap arm has a terminal-envelope pull-count
bound at every finite horizon. Integrating the resulting finite-horizon tail
gives an expected pseudo-regret bound with the explicit failure contribution;
it does not assert average consistency for a fixed nonzero `delta`.
