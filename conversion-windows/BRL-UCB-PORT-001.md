## Natural-Language Statement

For a finite stochastic bandit with sub-Gaussian rewards, UCB chooses the arm
with maximal empirical mean plus a confidence width.  The expected regret is
bounded by a logarithmic pull-count term for suboptimal arms plus summable bad
event terms.

## Route Decision

Current route: local source-faithful port. The native Real path-dependent index,
history mapping, generic fixed-count peeling, complete-stream law transport,
and next-unused-coordinate reward source compile under ABRL's current
toolchain. LML remains a theorem card rather than a dependency. The next
route-specific leaf is the recursive source-faithful UCB action and canonical
stationary/product stream law, not another deterministic confidence-proxy
wrapper.
