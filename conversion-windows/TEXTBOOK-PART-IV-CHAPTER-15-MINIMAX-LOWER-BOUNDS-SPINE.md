## Precise restatement

For two finite-armed bandits `nu=(P_i)` and `nu'=(P'_i)`, the laws generated by
one common randomized nonanticipating policy through time `n` satisfy

`D(P_nu^pi,P_nu'^pi) = sum_i E_nu^pi[T_i(n)] D(P_i,P'_i)`.

For every policy and `k>1`, `n>=k-1`, one of the unit-variance Gaussian mean
vectors in the source base/one-coordinate-change construction lies in
`[0,1]^k` and has expected regret at least
`sqrt((k-1)n)/27`. The minimax statement follows by taking the infimum over
policies and supremum over this environment class.
