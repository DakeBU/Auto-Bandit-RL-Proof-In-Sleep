# Open Problem Proposals

Use this directory for proposed proof-technology tasks before they become
active task packets.

Keep three statuses separate:

1. **literature status** — open, partially resolved, resolved, negatively resolved, or under source audit;
2. **formalization status** — missing, external, bridge-only, locally compiled, or blocked;
3. **task status** — proposal, active packet, accepted, or archived.

A missing local Lean theorem is not evidence that the mathematical problem is
open. Conversely, a solved mathematical problem may remain a high-priority
formalization task when its proof introduces a useful bridge, shortcut, hub, or
re-organization of the existing Lean graph.

The public problem-history registry lives at
`website/public-repo/data/frontier-problems.json`. Informal problems first seen
in posts/talks must preserve author, date, stable URL, a faithful statement and
an evidence grade before promotion to theorem-level comparison.
