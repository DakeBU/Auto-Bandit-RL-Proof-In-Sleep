Frontier & Open Problems Registry

A frontier problem remains useful after it is solved. We preserve the path from the original question through partial progress and resolution, then ask what mathematical nodes and edges the resolution adds to the existing BanditRLlib graph.

Three independent statuses. Mathematical openness, publication/review state, and Lean formalization state are never collapsed. “No resolution found in this targeted audit” is not a proof that a problem is still open.

Admission rule for posts, talks and social media

We may index an open problem first seen in a post or talk, but the record must preserve the original URL, author, date, exact problem statement or a short faithful paraphrase, and an evidence grade. Before it is promoted to a theorem-comparison case, a primary paper or an author-controlled source should freeze the mathematical contract. This prevents viral summaries from silently changing quantifiers or assumptions.
