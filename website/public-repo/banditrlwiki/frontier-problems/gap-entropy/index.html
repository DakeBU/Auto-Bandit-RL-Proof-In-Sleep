P0 frontier-history case · Best-arm identification

Gap-Entropy Conjecture

A decade-long fixed-confidence best-arm-identification problem is a near-ideal test of BanditRLlib's frontier philosophy: preserve the old question, the partial route, the new resolution, the formal proof object, and the structural change to the proof graph.

resolved in 2026 external Lean formalization local port: P0

Problem history

2016 · posed

Lijie Chen and Jian Li posed almost instance-wise optimality and the gap-entropy conjecture for BEST-1-ARM.

COLT 2016 open-problem source

2017 · major partial progress

Chen, Li and Qiao obtained a highly nontrivial upper route and a lower bound for restricted Gaussian/gap structure, but did not close the full conjecture.

COLT 2017 paper

2026 · two resolution routes meet

Yao, Zhao and Zhou released a manuscript together with a Lean 4 formalization. Aronow, Kallus and Lopatto independently posted a positive resolution on arXiv on 9 September 2026.

GapEntropy repository · independent arXiv resolution

Exact model boundary

The resolution should not be summarized as “BAI is solved.” The formalized target is fixed-confidence best-arm identification with independent unit-variance Gaussian arms, means in [0,1], a unique best arm, and an order-oblivious/permutation-averaged instance benchmark. The external formalization also documents explicit positive conventions for the iterated-log term where a literal source expression becomes negative or undefined.

QuantityRole
Δ_i = μ* - μ_iGap of a suboptimal arm.
H(I) = Σ Δ_i⁻²Classical inverse-gap-squared instance complexity.
p_rFraction of H contributed by arms in dyadic gap group r.
Ent(I) = Σ p_r log(1/p_r)Extra complexity measuring how H is spread across gap scales.
benchmarkOptimal expected sample complexity after averaging over permutations of arm labels.

External Lean evidence

The external repository is Apache-2.0, reports zero sorrys in the main formalization metadata, and records the standard foundational axioms propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. Its formalization.yaml maps source statements to Lean declarations.

GapEntropy.gapEntropyConjecture

Constant-factor characterization of the order-oblivious benchmark by H(I)(log(1/δ)+Ent(I)) under the formalized conventions.

GapEntropy.universalEntropyUpperBound

A single δ-correct algorithm with an explicit universal bound including the smallest-gap iterated-log correction.

GapEntropy.almostInstanceWiseOptimality

Almost instance-wise optimality with the manuscript's smooth positive iterated-log convention.

GapEntropy.positiveSourceAlmostInstanceWiseOptimality

Positive max-with-one truncation aligned to the source expression.

GapEntropy.PolicyRepresentations.standardBenchmark_eq

Policy-representation theorem equating the standard-Borel-private-randomness benchmark with the Gaussian-tape representation.

Not a BanditRLlib local compilation claim. GapEntropy pins Lean 4.33.1; this repository pins Lean 4.29.1. The first integration is therefore an attributed external graph bridge. A local source port is a separate P0 task.

Why this changes the graph

generic probability / adaptive histories / stopping ↓ change-of-measure & information lower bounds ↓ order-oblivious instance benchmark ↓ dyadic gap grouping → normalized H-mass → gap entropy ↓ benchmark lower/upper characterization ↓ universal fixed-confidence algorithm ↓ almost instance-wise optimality

If we only stored the final theorem, this would look like “another BAI sample-complexity result.” The graph view shows something stronger: the resolution inserts a reusable complexity coordinate—entropy of the distribution of inverse-gap-squared mass over scales—and a representation layer for what counts as an algorithm/benchmark. Those are candidate new hubs, not just terminal leaves.

P0 local-port checklist

  1. Freeze the external commit, Apache-2.0 attribution, toolchain and exact theorem signatures.
  2. Retrieve BanditRLlib's existing probability kernels, stopping-time, KL/testing and lower-bound declarations by exact type; classify reuse versus conceptual overlap.
  3. Choose whether to port the theorem into Lean 4.29.1, upgrade the local toolchain, or keep a versioned external proof certificate. Do not silently mix environments.
  4. Only after a local kernel gate passes may external theorem nodes be promoted from source/external to compiled local.
  5. Measure graph delta: new nodes, bridges, reused ancestors, newly-created hubs and any proof shortcuts relative to the existing fixed-confidence BAI route.
