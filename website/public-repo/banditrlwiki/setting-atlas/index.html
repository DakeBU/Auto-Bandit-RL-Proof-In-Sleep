Bandit & RL Setting Atlas

The old Setting Atlas is intentionally coarse. This registry is the canonical coverage baseline underneath it: settings, objectives, method families, cross-cutting guarantees and application bridges are separated rather than flattened into one alphabetic list.

Normalization matters. Thompson sampling and GP-UCB are methods, BAI and regret minimization are objectives, and LLM is an application bridge. “Bolling Bandits” is retained as an alias of Ballooning Bandits. OMDP, SLB and Transform remain quarantined until a cited source fixes their intended expansion.

Maintenance rule

A new term should not become a comparable result case until its model contract fixes environment/action class, feedback, objective, probability mode, parameters, computation/oracle assumptions, upper/lower bound and source theorem. A missing Lean formalization is not automatically a mathematical open problem.
