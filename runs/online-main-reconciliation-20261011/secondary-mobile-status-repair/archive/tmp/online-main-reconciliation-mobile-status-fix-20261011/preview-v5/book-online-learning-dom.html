    Online Learning Book

    Orabona v10 shares one canonical Lean library: Chapter 1 foundations, extended convexity and supporting gradients, projected OGD with fixed or decreasing steps, and subsequent bounded Chapter 2 packages. Probability, EXP3 and Tsallis references remain shared. Chapter 2 and the Chapters 1-16 program remain incomplete.
