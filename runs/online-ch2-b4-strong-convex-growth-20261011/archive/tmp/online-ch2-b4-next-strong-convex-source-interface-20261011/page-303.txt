Definition A.2A topological spaceXis aHausdorff spaceif any two distinct points can
be separated by disjoint open sets. That is, for anyx,y∈ Xwithx̸=y, there exist open
setsU,V ⊆ Xsuch thatx∈ U,y∈ V, andU ∩ V=∅.
Note that Euclidean spaces are Hausdorff spaces.
Theorem A.3(Weierstrass theorem for extended-real-valued functions (Bauschke et al.,
2003, Theorem 1.29))LetXbe a Hausdorff space, letf:X →[−∞,+∞]be lower
semicontinuous, and letVbe a compact subset ofX. SupposeV ∩domf̸=∅. Then,f
achieves its infimum overV.
