Remark 7.2We basically did not assume anything onℓ t nor onψ t, hence the above
equality holds even for non-convex losses and regularizers. Yet, solving the minimization
problem at each step might be computationally infeasible.

hence we can always state the regret guarantee withψ t(u)−min x∈V ψt(x)instead of
