This is an interesting result on its own: it gives a principled way to set the learning rates
with an almost optimal guarantee. Observe that this approach avoids knowing the Lipschitz
constants of the functions, so the algorithm adapts to them. The downside is that it works
only in the bounded case. Another important observation is that the sum of the squared
gradients acts as anintrinsic notion of time, better suited thanTto capture the dependence
on time.
