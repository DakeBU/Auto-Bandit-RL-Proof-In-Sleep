The approach I will follow is toreduce the OLO game to an online game of betting on a
coin, where the lower bounds are easier to prove. So, let’s introduce the coin-betting online
game:
•Start with an initial amount of moneyϵ >0.
•In each round, the algorithm bets a fraction of its current wealth on the outcome of a coin.
•The outcome of the coin is revealed, and the algorithm wins or loses its bet at even odds.
The aim of this online game is to win as much money as possible. Also, as in all the online
games we consider, we do not assume anything about how the outcomes of the coin are
decided.
