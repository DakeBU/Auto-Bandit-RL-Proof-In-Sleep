The unique subgradient of a differentiable function is just the gradient, as quantified in
the next theorem.
Theorem 2.22(Rockafellar, 1970, Theorem 25.1)If the functionf:R d →[−∞,+∞]
is convex and finite inx, it is differentiable inxiff the subdifferential consists of a single
element, that turns out to be∇f(x).
