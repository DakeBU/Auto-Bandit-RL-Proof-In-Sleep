   Now we have a well-defined algorithm, but does it guarantee a sublinear regret? We know
that at least in one case, it recovers the OSD algorithm, which does work. So, from an intu-
itive point of view, how well the algorithm works should depend on some characteristic of
ψ . In particular, a sufficient property will be the strong convexity of ψ . The strong convexity
also takes care of the existence of the argmin in the algorithm, thanks to the next theorem.
