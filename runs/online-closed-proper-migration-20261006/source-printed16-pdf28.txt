2.2 Online Subgradient Descent
In the previous section, we introduced Projected Online Gradient Descent. However, the
differentiability assumption for theℓ t is quite strong. What happens when the losses are
convex but not differentiable? For example,ℓt(x) =|x−10|. Note that this situation is more
common than one would think. For example, the hinge loss,ℓt(x) = max(1−yt⟨zt,x⟩,0),
is not differentiable. It turns out that we can just use OGD, substitutingsubgradientsfor the
gradients. For this, we need some more convex analysis.
