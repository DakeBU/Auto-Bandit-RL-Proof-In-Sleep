    Notation you will meet

    Two separate models
    Finite-dimensional real WHOLE SPACE only; uniform positive c, old x=c*y and transformed loss f(c*y), initial/comparator divided by c, eta/c^2, selected c*g. Conditional additive dimension-exponent consistency gives eta unit [x]^2/[loss] and regret unit [loss]; this is not a physical-unit type checker or numerical performance theorem. Loss units remain unchanged. No arbitrary constrained-domain projection, anisotropic/affine coordinate or independent choice-function equivariance theorem.
    Transported policy
    The SAME exogenous deterministic finite-history policy is explicitly adapted: inverse-transform past/current whole losses, multiply played history by c, query original policy, multiply selected answer by c. It receives finite past loss functions/played outputs/current whole loss; exogenous parameter selection itself is not restricted by this function type. ALL natural-round history/output/selected correspondences are derived from the actual shared whole-space nearest-projection recurrence. Arbitrary schedules/structural identities need no positive eta or legal policy; performance requires its separate premises.
    Boundary
    Proper means no bottom anywhere and some finite real value. A proper loss/global EReal support at c*y yields a scaled global support; this is a one-way implication, not exact subdifferential-set equality. Prefix properness plus actual old on-path legality produce actual scaled legality, with no off-path oracle law. Bare EReal.toReal regret equality for unrestricted losses is total algebra, not a finite-loss guarantee. The sharp fixed-positive-step theorem requires whole-space SubdifferentiableOn losses and actual legal played supports, which ensure finite played/comparator values; it retains the SAME original-run negative terminal squared-distance residual. The two real gradient-chainrule helpers allow ANY real c under their HasGradientAt/DifferentiableAt hypotheses; invertible loss/path transport requires c>0. c0 inverse identity is inadmissible and fails in the named example; c1 is identity. The fixed eta>0 sharp endpoint includes T0 with exact cancellation. Source known-horizon1/sqrtT requires natural T>=1, and totalized1/sqrt0=0 is not a positive tuned schedule. No anytime, future-informed energy optimizer or minimax/lower-bound certificate.
