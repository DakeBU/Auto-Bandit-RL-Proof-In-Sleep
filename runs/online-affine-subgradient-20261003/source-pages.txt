Theorem 2.28 Let f : Rm → (−∞, +∞] be proper. Define h(x) = f (Ax + b), where
A ∈ Rm×d and b ∈ Rm . Then, we have A⊤ ∂f (Ax + b) ⊆ ∂h(x).
Proof For any g ∈ ∂f (Ax + b), we want to show that A⊤ g is a subgradient of h in x.

               h(y) = f (Ay + b) ≥ f (Ax + b) + ⟨g, Ay + b − (Ax + b)⟩

that implies our stated result.
