Another critical property of convex functions is Jensen’s inequality.
Theorem 2.9(Jensen’s inequality)Letf:R d →(−∞,+∞]be a measurable convex
function andxbe anR d-valued random element on some probability space such thatE[x]
exists andx∈domfwith probability 1. Then, we have
E[f(x)]≥f(E[x]).
We can now see our first OCO algorithm in the case that the functions are convex and
differentiable.
