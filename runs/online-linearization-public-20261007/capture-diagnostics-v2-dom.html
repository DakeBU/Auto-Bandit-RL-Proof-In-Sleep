    Reader checkpoint

    Notation you will meet

    Learner A and generated vectors
    A_t receives exactly t prior vectors. Current p sees past whole losses, reconstructed played outputs and current whole loss after X_t is determined.
    Same outputs
    X_t equals A_t on the generated prefix; the linear loss is inner(g_t,x) and both regrets evaluate exactly these X_t.
    Source and library boundary
    Finite-dimensional real Euclidean space and shared nonempty closed convex V; no boundedness. Proper EReal loss excludes bottom and has a finite ambient witness; global supports on V and actual played supports justify finite played/comparator values before toReal. Round1=Lean0; T0 is an algebraic extension. Comparator and horizon are evaluation parameters, not policy inputs.
