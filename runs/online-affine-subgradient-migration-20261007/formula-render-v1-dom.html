    Affine transport of subgradients

    Transport actual global supports through an affine map and its adjoint; properness alone, inclusion only.

    Who should read this. Readers of Orabona Theorem2.28.

    Learning goals

    • Translate the all-point support inequality through Ay+b.
    • Use the actual inner-product adjoint of A.
    • Distinguish inclusion from equality and keep the proper nonconvex case.

    Transport an actual support

    Read top to bottom: each step supplies the state or proof fact used by the next one.

    1. 01
      Choose a source support

      Every image element is actual A*g from the full global source support.

    2. 02
      Test every affine image point

      Apply hg(Ay+b) to every y and cancel translation by map_sub.

    3. 03
      Use the adjoint identity

      Use actual adjoint_inner_left; obtain target all-y support inclusion, never equality.

    Proof bridge · source to Lean

    Global support transported by the genuine adjoint

    Unpack each actual image witness g. Its global inequality is tested at Ay+b for EVERY y. Cancel b and rewrite (Ay+b)-(Ax+b)=A(y-x) with actual map_sub; actual adjoint_inner_left turns inner(g,A(y-x)) into inner(A*g,y-x). This same EReal inequality is exactly the target full global-support predicate. No supplied pullback formula or selected-support shortcut.
