    proof flow · ordered flow

    Prove both directions of the maximum rule

    Read top to bottom: each step supplies the state or proof fact used by the next one.

    1. 01
      Lift active supports

      An attaining component has fi(x)=F(x) and fi(y)≤F(y) for every ambient y. Its full global support therefore supports F. The maximum support set is convex at this finite query, so the ordinary active convex hull supports F. The active lifting result needs no properness/convexity/continuity; hull-forward needs properness/common finite query but not convexity of the fi or continuity. Those weaker-premise foundations are not the full numbered source terminal.

    2. 02
      Establish ordinary hull compactness

      Ambient continuity and finite query values give actual interior neighborhoods of the effective domains. Shared local support boundedness and closed global inequalities make component supports compact. A convex join is the continuous image of [0,1] times two compact sets; finite induction gives compactness of the ordinary active hull, with inactive sets empty. The generic finite-convex-union helper permits an empty selected Finset; the domain-interior continuity helper permits a bottom query value, unlike the source terminal.

    3. 03
      Construct the reverse

      For g supporting F and EACH direction d, use yn=x+d/(n+1), choose actual maximizing indices and actual component supports at sufficiently large n, and use the finite family local bounds for a compact subsequence. One finite index recurs; the restricted frequent-index filter is NeBot. Continuity makes that index active at x, and the global support limit yields k in its full support set. Actual displacement comparison gives inner(g,d)≤inner(k,d). This k depends on d, including d=0; no single k for all directions and no supplied witness is claimed. If g were outside the ordinary active hull, its compactness and convexity give strict separation by a continuous linear functional. Riesz supplies the corresponding direction d. The constructed active k has at least the directional value of g, contradicting separation. Thus every maximum support belongs to the ordinary hull; both equality directions are proved without a closure replacement.
