V_H^pi(s)=0, and for t=0,...,H-1, V_t^pi(s)=sum_a pi_t(a|s)[r(s,a)+sum_s' P(s'|s,a)V_{t+1}^pi(s')]. The terminal condition anchors backward evaluation; without it the same recursions admit different solutions. Terminal payoff g changes V_H to g and propagates its expected value backward. With one state, H=1, actions of rewards 0 and 1, and the policy always choosing the zero-reward action, V_0^pi=0 while max_a r(s,a)=1. Maximization evaluates optimal control instead of the given policy. Finite backward recursion with fixed terminal data needs no strict discount/contraction assumption; undiscounted sums terminate after H decisions.
