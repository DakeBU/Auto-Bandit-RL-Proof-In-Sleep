Backward induction starts with U_H=V_H=0. If U_{t+1}=V_{t+1} pointwise, the same B_t applied to equal functions gives U_t=B_t U_{t+1}=B_t V_{t+1}=V_t. This covers all t<=H, including H=0. Stochastic transition averaging is at most nonexpansive in sup norm in the undiscounted case, not necessarily strictly contractive; a one-state self-loop with zero reward has Bf=f. For any H>=1 in this model, U_t=0 for all t and V_t=1 for all t both satisfy every recursion, but have different terminal data. This shows why terminal data cannot be dropped. In finite horizon, backward induction suffices. Infinite horizon lacks a terminal base case; discounting or other additional structure is needed for a corresponding uniqueness claim. The Lean task compares an arbitrary recursively specified v to the library valueAt construction, which already has the zero terminal condition.
