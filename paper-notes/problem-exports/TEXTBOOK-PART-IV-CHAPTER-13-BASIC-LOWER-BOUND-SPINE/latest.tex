\paragraph{Model and notation.}
For a fixed-horizon expected pseudo-regret functional $R$, policy class
$\mathcal P$, and environment class $\mathcal E$, the worst-case value is
$\sup_{\nu\in\mathcal E}R(\pi,\nu)$ and the minimax value is its infimum over
$\pi\in\mathcal P$. A minimax-optimal policy is admissible and attains this
infimum for the same classes and horizon-indexed functional.

For the two-point test in Section~13.1, the distribution-level observation law
is $\mathcal N(\mu,1/n)$. The midpoint decision returns $\Delta$ when the
observation is at least $\Delta/2$ and zero otherwise.
The canonical finite product of $n>0$ copies of $\mathcal N(\mu,1)$ is mapped
by the arithmetic mean to exactly this observation law.
