\paragraph{Proof map.}
For every $p>0$, consistency gives $R_n/n^p\to0$.  Addition preserves this
limit, so the sum of the original- and alternative-environment regrets is
eventually at most $n^p$.  When the sum is positive, monotonicity of logarithm
and $\log(n^p)=p\log n$ give
\[
  \frac{\log(R_n+R_n')}{\log n}\le p
\]
eventually.  Separately, $d_{\inf}$ is an extended-real infimum of
$D(P\Vert P')$ over strictly better alternatives.  Inserting any admissible
alternative therefore upper-bounds the infimum.  For unit-variance Gaussians,
the mean $\mu^\star+\varepsilon$ is admissible when $\varepsilon>0$, and the
compiled Gaussian KL identity gives cost
$(\mu^\star-\mu+\varepsilon)^2/2$.
